Nahmanides on the verse, in Charles Chavel's translation (Shilo, 1971-1976;
the Deuteronomy volume is dated 1976 here on the series dates Sefaria records,
which declares the translation CC-BY). He reports the reading of "the
commentators" (Ibn Ezra's: secret idolatry, punished by God) and the Rabbis'
(Sanhedrin 43b), then gives his own *peshat*: the hidden things are sins hidden
from the sinner himself, unwitting sins, for which the covenant's curse does
not fall, with Psalm 19:13 and Onkelos's "before the Eternal" as his evidence.

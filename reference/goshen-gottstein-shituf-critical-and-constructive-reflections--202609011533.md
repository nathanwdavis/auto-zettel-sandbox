Bibliographic record all the same, because the abstract states a position this
repository needs: that shituf is a serviceable *internal* Jewish category and a
poor *description* of the religion it is applied to. Goshen-Gottstein's book
argues the case with Hinduism, but the abstract itself says the move was
"previously made in relation to Christianity", which is the case the same-God
cluster here is built on.

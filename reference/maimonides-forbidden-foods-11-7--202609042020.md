Bibliographic record. The halachah in which Maimonides classifies gentiles by
whether they serve false deities, for the purposes of the wine prohibition, and
names the Arabs as an instance of those who do not.

The printed text here is **censored**, and the translator says so in his own
note: the standard version's colourless "those who worship false deities" stands
where the uncensored text reads "Christians, by contrast, are idolaters." Notes
citing this reference must distinguish the two, and this record exists partly to
make that distinction citable. Touger's note also points to the Rema at Orach
Chayim 155:1 for the later softening of the Christian case to shituf.

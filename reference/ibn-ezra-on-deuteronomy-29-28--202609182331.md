Ibn Ezra on the verse, Hebrew (public domain as Sefaria declares it) and the
Strickman-Silver English (CC-BY-NC). The year is the close of the series dates
Sefaria records (1988-2004); the Deuteronomy volume's own year was not
verified this cycle. Two things are here: the plain reading (the hidden thing
is one who serves idols in secret, whose punishment is in God's hands, while
public sin obliges "us and our children"), and a rival reading he reports and
rejects, that both the hidden and the revealed belong to God and ours is only
to do the Torah, of which he says it "has no head or foot". Captured for the
2026-09-15 leads entry on Deuteronomy 29:28.

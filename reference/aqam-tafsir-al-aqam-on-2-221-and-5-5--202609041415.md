Bibliographic record for *Tafsir al-A'qam* on Q 2:221 and Q 5:5. Arabic,
verbatim, in full, at
`raw/202609041415-aqam-tafsir-al-aqam-2-221-and-5-5-arabic.txt`. The second of
the two Zaydi works this cycle read, and the one that carries the result.

**Why this source.** *Gharib al-Qur'an* glosses a word and stops. A test of the
claim "*some* Zaydis hold X" needs a commentary that records positions and says
whose they are, and al-A'qam is that: a late-classical Yemeni Zaydi commentary
that reports the school's own imams by name. On Q 5:5 it attributes a reading to
`يحيى والقاسم (عليهم السلام)` — Yahya al-Hadi ila al-Haqq, founder of the Zaydi
imamate in Yemen, and al-Qasim al-Rassi, the two figures a Zaydi work cites the
way an Imami work cites al-Baqir and al-Sadiq.

**Date, stated as loosely as the host states it.** altafsir.com dates him only
`ت القرن 9 هـ`, "d. 9th century AH", and names no year. `issued` above carries
1400 CE as the century's rough midpoint so that a citation renders; it is not a
death date and nothing in this base turns on it. No closer date is claimed than
the host supplies.

**Section boundaries, and why the capture carries more verses than its title.**
This host serves a section per ayah, but a commentary that treats several verses
in one passage is served whole under each of them. The Q 2:221 section therefore
runs on into Q 2:222-223 and the Q 5:5 section opens with Q 5:4. Nothing was
trimmed: the surrounding material is part of the served text and is left where
the commentator put it. Locators in the literature note name the verse actually
being commented on.

**Access and pagination.** altafsir.com, `tMadhNo=5`, `tTafsirNo=47`; one
content page per section, read to exhaustion under the corrected rule and probed
two pages past the end, as recorded in the capture header.

**Edition.** None named by the host, none claimed; locators are by sura:aya.

**One host only,** and as with the other Zaydi capture, no host known to this
base carries Zaydi tafsir at all besides this one. The attribution to Yahya and
al-Qasim is a positive quotation from a single institutional host, and it is
worth a second witness when one becomes reachable; that is filed to INBOX rather
than left implicit.

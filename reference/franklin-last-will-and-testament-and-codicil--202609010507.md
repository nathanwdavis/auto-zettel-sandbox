Bibliographic record. Benjamin Franklin's last will (17 July 1788) with the
codicil signed 23 June 1789, as printed in the standard Smyth edition of
Franklin's writings, vol. X (Macmillan, 1907), pp. 493–510; the codicil
occupies pp. 501–510. The codicil is the primary source for Franklin's
two-century compounding bequest: £1,000 each to Boston and Philadelphia,
lent at 5 per cent to young married artificers, with staged projections of
£131,000 at one hundred years and £4,061,000 at two hundred.

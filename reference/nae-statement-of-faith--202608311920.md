Bibliographic record. The NAE is the principal cross-denominational body of
US evangelicalism, and this seven-article statement is the doctrinal basis it
requires of members, which is why it is used here as a representative
*mainstream evangelical* answer rather than the confession of any one
denomination. The NAE states on the page that it has intentionally not
copyrighted the statement; it is captured in full. No adoption date is given
on the page, so the CSL entry carries the access year only.

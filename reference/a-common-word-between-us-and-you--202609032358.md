Bibliographic record. An open letter of 2007 C.E. / 1428 A.H. from Muslim
scholars, clerics and intellectuals to the leaders of the Christian churches,
published by The Royal Aal al-Bayt Institute for Islamic Thought in Amman,
Jordan, which carries the copyright line. It is usually referred to as the
letter of 138 Muslim scholars, after its original signatories.

Filed as `primary-text` rather than `reputable-secondary` deliberately: this is
not a report about what Muslim theologians think, it is the statement itself,
issued collectively and in its signatories' own name. That is precisely what
the same-God cluster in this base was missing.

Captured from the initiative's own site (acommonword.com, HTTP 200,
2026-09-03), not from a third-party reproduction. The capture is an EXCERPT —
the passages bearing on the same-God question, plus enough of the framing to
show what kind of document it is — and not the complete letter; the two long
expository sections on the two commandments within each scripture are
represented only in part. Anything resting on the unexcerpted portions needs a
fuller capture first.

The page has no pagination, so locators for this source are section names:
"Summary and Abridgement", "(I) LOVE OF GOD", "(II) LOVE OF THE NEIGHBOUR",
"(III) COME TO A COMMON WORD BETWEEN US AND YOU".

Two things this record does not establish. The signatory list was not captured,
so the count of 138 is reported as the letter's standard description rather
than verified from the page. And the document is in English on this site;
whether an Arabic original governs it was not determined. Neither bears on the
passages actually cited from it in this base, all of which are quoted from the
English text as captured.

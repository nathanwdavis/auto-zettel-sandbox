Bibliographic record. The first canon of the Council of Constantinople (381),
which anathematizes a list of heresies by party name, in the NPNF second-series
English (1900). Public domain; the capture is the whole page, canon and
editorial notes together.

A canon is a disciplinary decree of the council, distinct from its creed. The
distinction is load-bearing for the notes that cite this: what is anathematized
here is not anathematized in any creed.

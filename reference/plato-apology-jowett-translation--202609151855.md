Plato's Apology in Benjamin Jowett's translation (first published 1871; public
domain) as served by the Internet Classics Archive at MIT, whose page is
captured verbatim as HTML. The Archive does not say which of Jowett's editions
it prints, so the year recorded is the first. Captured for inquiry
202609151845 for one passage, the Delphi story (Stephanus 21a-d), where the
whole of Socratic wisdom is put as knowing what one does not know -- the
oldest statement on file of what the owner's note calls a known unknown.

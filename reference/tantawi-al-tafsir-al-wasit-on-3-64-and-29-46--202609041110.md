Bibliographic record for the two sections of Muhammad Sayyid Tantawi's *al-Tafsir
al-Wasit li-l-Qur'an al-Karim* that this cycle captured: the commentary on Q 3:64
and on Q 29:46. Arabic, verbatim, in full, at
`raw/202609041110-tantawi-al-wasit-3-64-and-29-46-arabic.txt`.

**Why this source: it closes a standing item rather than opening a new one.**
The 08:00Z cycle found Tantawi's gloss of the shared-God clause on the quran.com
endpoint (resource 93) and deliberately declined to build on it, because it
stood on ONE witness and no second host could be found. The 09:00Z cycle then
settled the search negatively for the hosts it knew: tafsir.app's embedded
165-entry resource list contains no *al-Wasit* under any spelling, and
quran.ksu.edu.sa carries five tafsirs, none of them his. The INBOX entry left
the item open in one sentence — "find one independent host and the note gains a
witness for the cost of one request". This cycle found two: altafsir.com carries
him at `tMadhNo=7&tTafsirNo=57`, and quranpedia.net carries him as book 321.

**Access.** altafsir.com, both verses on the first request, section exhausted at
`Page=2`. The gloss is on page 1 of the 29:46 section.

**Cross-check on an independent host, done mechanically.** quranpedia.net book
321 (محمد سيد طنطاوي), whole-sura page
`https://quranpedia.net/surah/1/29/book/321`. The operative gloss matches
verbatim across the two hosts, differing only in the orthographic variant
`فى`/`في`, which normalisation folds.

**The cross-check also corrects the base.** INBOX recorded, from the quran.com
copy, that Tantawi's lemma "drops `وإلهنا`" — that he glosses only
`وإلهكم واحد`. Both hosts captured here read the full lemma
`وَإِلهُنا وَإِلهُكُمْ واحِدٌ`. The truncation is an artifact of the quran.com
resource-93 copy, not a feature of Tantawi's text, and any reading built on it
would have been a reading of the host rather than of the commentator. Recorded
here because it is the second time in three cycles that a single host's
rendering of this cluster proved to be the host's and not the author's.

**Edition, stated honestly.** No printed edition is named by either host and no
page markers are served; none is claimed and locators are by sura:aya. The
issued year in the CSL record is the date of the standard Nahdat Misr edition
and is bibliographic background, not something this capture establishes.

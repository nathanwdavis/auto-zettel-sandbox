Bibliographic record. The standard monograph on Leon Modena's *Ari Nohem*
(Venice, 1639), the first sustained Jewish critique of Kabbalah — and this
repository's route to the Jewish side of the dispute that reference
[[pico-conclusiones-sive-theses-dccccc--202609011801]] records from the
Christian side only.

Tiered `reputable-secondary` rather than `peer-reviewed`: a scholarly monograph
from Princeton University Press in a refereed series, but a book, not a
refereed article. That tier is also the honest one for how it is used here.
**This is a scholar reporting Modena's argument, not Modena's own text.**
*Ari Nohem* itself is not in this repository, in Hebrew or in translation, and
no note may quote Modena or attribute wording to him on the strength of this
reference. What the notes may say is what Dweck says Modena concluded. Same
class of limit as the Goshen-Gottstein record, and it should be closed the same
way, by capturing the primary text — see INBOX.

Captured from the publisher's own free sample-chapter PDF of the introduction,
served from Princeton's assets host and watermarked "Copyrighted Material" on
every page, so the capture is bounded excerpts: four blocks from pp. 9-11.

One normalization is substantive and is recorded in the capture header rather
than buried: the PDF's text layer renders many capitals in lowercase
("nahmanides", "latin"), an artifact of the font encoding and not of the
printed page. Capitalization was restored to standard form in the captured
blocks. The wording, which is what the notes turn on, is verbatim under both
pdfminer.six and poppler.

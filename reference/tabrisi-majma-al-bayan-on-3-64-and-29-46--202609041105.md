Bibliographic record for the two sections of Abu 'Ali al-Fadl ibn al-Hasan
al-Tabrisi's *Majma' al-bayan fi tafsir al-Qur'an* that this cycle captured: the
commentary on Q 3:64 and on Q 29:46. Arabic, verbatim, in full, at
`raw/202609041105-tabrisi-majma-al-bayan-3-64-and-29-46-arabic.txt`.

**Why this source.** It is the second half of the Shi'i test the 10:00Z cycle
named — al-Tusi's *Tibyan* and al-Tabrisi's *Majma' al-bayan* were named
together, and taking only one would have left the result resting on a single
commentator. *Majma' al-bayan* is also the more widely read of the two, and it
is the one an Imami reader is most likely to meet.

**Access.** altafsir.com, `tMadhNo=4&tTafsirNo=3`, both verses on the first
request, section exhausted at `Page=2`. The gloss this cycle was looking for is
on page 2, not page 1 — see the capture header on why that matters.

**Edition, stated honestly.** No printed edition is named by the host and the
served body carries no page markers; none is claimed and locators are by
sura:aya.

**One host only.** As for the *Tibyan*, and for the same reasons: the eight
rungs tried and their failure modes are recorded in the capture header. What is
taken from this capture is a positive quotation.

**A caution that belongs on this record rather than in a note.** The operative
gloss here is word-for-word identical to al-Tusi's on the same clause
(`لا شريك له`), and al-Tabrisi wrote a century after al-Tusi. That the two are
textually related is a commonplace of the literature, but this repository has
captured no source that says so, so the notes built on these two treat them as
two attestations of one reading rather than as two independent witnesses to it,
and say so. Establishing the dependence from a source is queued in INBOX.

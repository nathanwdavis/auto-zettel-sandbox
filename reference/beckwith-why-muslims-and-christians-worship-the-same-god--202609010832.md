Bibliographic record. Beckwith's follow-up, three weeks after his December 17
column drew critical replies from Mohler, Walker, Cochran, and Leithart. It
restates the co-reference argument on the general-revelation/special-revelation
distinction: natural-theological argument (his example is the kalam argument,
which he attributes to al-Ghazali) delivers a thin description of the one
Creator; the traditions' incompatible claims are additions from rival special
revelations, and error there does not sever reference. Carries the
Abdullah thought experiment and the Lois Lane / Lana Lang case. Editors'
preface and full article body captured verbatim.

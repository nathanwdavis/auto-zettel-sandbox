Bibliographic record. **Abstract only** -- the article is open access at Faith
and Philosophy's official repository, but the PDF (and its PhilPapers and
PhilArchive mirrors) sits behind a Cloudflare bot challenge this environment
cannot pass, and the Wayback copy is blocked by the egress policy; the full
text has not been read. Notes citing it are limited to what the abstract itself
states: the reference-theoretic framing (Gareth Evans's dossier/dominance
account), the "it depends" conclusion, and the two-part counterfactual test.
Verified against Crossref by DOI. This is the most-cited analytic-philosophy
treatment of the same-God dispute and the natural professional counterpart to
Beckwith's popular columns.

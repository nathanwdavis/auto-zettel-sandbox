The Talmudic passage Rashi reports for his reading of Deuteronomy 29:28 (29:29
in English Bibles), captured for the leads entry the 2026-09-15 cycle filed.
Three segments only: the question why Israel was not punished for Achan's
hidden sin, Rabbi Yohanan's answer (not until the Jordan was crossed), and the
tannaitic dispute over the extraordinary points on "to us and to our children"
-- Rabbi Yehuda reading them as dating collective liability for hidden sins to
the crossing, Rabbi Nehemya denying that the nation is ever liable for
individuals' hidden sins and reading the dots of open sins instead. English is
the William Davidson (Koren-Steinsaltz) translation, CC-BY-NC as Sefaria
declares it, with the vocalized Aramaic. `scripture: false` on the Pirkei Avot
precedent: the Talmud is cited with a bibliography entry in this base.

English Dominican Province translation (Benziger, 1920; public domain) as
published by New Advent; the whole page (all thirteen articles) is captured
verbatim as HTML. This base already holds q.2 (existence), q.3 (simplicity)
and II-II q.103 (dulia) from the same host and translation. The articles this
cycle reads for inquiry 202609151845 are 1 (whether a created intellect can
see the essence of God), 7 (whether those who see it comprehend him), 8
(whether they see all things in it), 12 (what natural reason can know of God
in this life) and 13 (whether grace gives a higher knowledge).

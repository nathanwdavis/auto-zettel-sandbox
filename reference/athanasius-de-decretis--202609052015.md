Bibliographic record. Athanasius's Defence of the Nicene Definition, in the
Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers translation by Archibald Robertson (1900), public
domain. The work defends the Nicene Council's decrees and explains the doctrine
of the Son's homoousios (consubstantiality) with the Father.

translation conventions of 1900 patristic scholarship; claims about logical form
rest on the English rendering, not on access to the Greek.

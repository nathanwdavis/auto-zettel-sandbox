Bibliographic record. Maimonides on the Messianic king. Halachot 6 and 7 are the
passage the Christian censors removed from the printed editions; the Touger
translation restores them, and the capture keeps halachot 4, 6 and 7.

This is where Maimonides states his own verdict on Christianity and on Islam in
one breath, which is why it is on file here rather than only in a Messianic
context.

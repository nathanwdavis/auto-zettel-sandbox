Scripture, cited in-text per SBL and excluded from the bibliography (FR-9).
Genesis 4:7, 6:5 and 8:21 in Hebrew (Miqra according to the Masorah,
CC-BY-SA) and in the JPS Tanakh Gender-Sensitive Edition (CC-BY-NC), with
the KJV of 6:5: the three verses the rabbinic texts captured this cycle
read for the evil inclination. Captured so that which of them carries the
word yetzer, and which has the inclination read into it, can be checked in
the Hebrew rather than assumed.

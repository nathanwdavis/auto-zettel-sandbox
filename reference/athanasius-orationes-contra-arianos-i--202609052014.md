Bibliographic record. Athanasius's First Discourse Against the Arians, in the
Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers translation by Archibald Robertson (1900), public
domain. The work defends the Nicene position against Arian objections about the
nature of the Son's eternal generation.

not the sections quoted in associated literature notes. Tertullian.org provided
the working source, and no second independent transcription of the quoted passages
was obtained.

Robertson's English translation is a witness to the Greek text and carries the
translation conventions of 1900 patristic scholarship; claims about logical form
rest on the English rendering, not on access to the Greek.

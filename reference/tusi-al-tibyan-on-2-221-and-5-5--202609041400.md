Bibliographic record for the two sections of al-Tusi's *al-Tibyan* that this
cycle captured: the commentary on Q 2:221 and on Q 5:5. Arabic, verbatim, in
full, at `raw/202609041400-tusi-al-tibyan-2-221-and-5-5-arabic.txt`. This is a
second, separate capture from the same work; the earlier one, on Q 3:64 and
Q 29:46, is [[tusi-al-tibyan-on-3-64-and-29-46--202609041100]], and the two
notes carry different URLs because they are different sections.

**What this source was brought in to settle.** The 13:00Z cycle established from
al-Qurtubi and al-Sabuni that whether the People of the Book fall under the
Qur'an's *mushrikun* is a live juristic dispute, and that the strictest position
— the one on which Q 2:221 cancels Q 5:5 rather than the reverse — is attributed
to the Imamiyya and some Zaydis. That attribution rested on a single clause in a
single modern Sunni author, and the cycle that wrote it filed its own note to
INBOX saying so: *no Imami or Zaydi legal text is in the base at all*. This
capture and the three taken beside it are the answer to that entry.

**Why both verses and not just Q 2:221.** Al-Tusi does not finish the question on
Q 2:221. He states his position there and then defers the argument:
`وسنبين وجه الآية في المائدة اذا انتهينا إليها` — "we shall explain the sense of
the verse in al-Ma'ida when we come to it." Q 5:5 is where he redeems the note,
and it is where the *reasoning*, the two alternative *ta'wils*, and the two
Imami reports that invert the abrogation actually sit. Taking Q 2:221 alone
would have captured a claim without its grounds.

**Access.** altafsir.com, `tMadhNo=4` (Twelver Shi'i), `tTafsirNo=39`, the route
this base has used since the 11:00Z cycle and the only host in its experience
that carries Shi'i tafsir at all. Both verses returned on the first request. The
madhhab group, work and author were read back from the served page's own
selected options rather than assumed from the numeric ids.

**Pagination — the standing rule was wrong, and this capture is the first read
under the corrected one.** The base's access note said a section with a
successor page carries a `التالي` control. That is true of page 1 only: interior
pages render the same control with the English alt `Next`, and the control is
served indefinitely, on empty pages past the end of a section as well. Testing
for `التالي` caps every section at two pages. Q 5:5 here runs to three, so the
old rule would have taken this capture short — and would have cut it exactly
before the sentence that inverts the abrogation. Exhaustion is now established
from the text: each section was read until a page returned the verse alone, and
the two pages after the last were fetched and are recorded in the capture
header. The audit of what the old rule had already cost this base, and the two
pages it recovered, are in
`raw/202609041420-recovered-third-pages-tabrisi-and-maturidi-29-46-arabic.txt`.

**Edition, stated honestly.** altafsir.com names no printed edition for these
sections and the served body carries no page markers, so nothing keys the text
to a printed edition and none is claimed. All locators are by sura:aya.

**One host only.** No independent host for the *Tibyan* was found from this
environment; the eight rungs tried and their failure modes are recorded in the
earlier capture and are unchanged. What this repository takes from this capture
is positive quotation — positions al-Tusi states in his own voice and marks as
his school's, with `عندنا` — and a single institutional host is a weak threat to
that.

Bibliographic record. A refereed article in the *Journal of the Evangelical
Theological Society* 55/4 (2012), from the PDF the Society posts in its open
archive. Tiered `peer-reviewed` on the venue. The author note gives a private
address in Yakima, Washington, and no institution; the article's standing
here is that of a JETS article, which is what the tier records. It is the
fullest account on file of the exchange over "compatibilist middle
knowledge": it quotes Ware's *God's Greater Glory* (pp. 114-15, 115 n. 10,
123-24) and Tiessen's *Providence and Prayer* (p. 319), reports the
Helm-Tiessen conversation in *Westminster Theological Journal* 71 (2009) and
Ware's response in *Perspectives on the Doctrine of God* (2008), and gives
its own verdict. None of those works is on file; Ware's and Tiessen's words
enter this repository only as Van Horn quotes them, with his page numbers.

The article is in copyright (the Society and the author) and served openly
by its publisher, so the capture is bounded excerpts per the repository's
excerpt policy: ten blocks, 4,121 words of 11,895 (35 percent), each a
contiguous run of the PDF extraction and therefore including the footnotes
and running heads that fall inside it. Captured: the opening paragraph with
the two theses; section III (Tiessen, Ware, Feinberg); the paragraph on how
odd the claims are, and the Westminster Confession paragraph; section IV
through the concession; the conclusion of section IV with Tiessen's lesson;
the Ware "occasioning" passage in section V with note 59; and the first and
third paragraphs of section VII. NOT captured: section I (definitions),
section II (the doctrine), the Turretin, Bavinck and Muller quotations, the
premotion and causal-powers argument, the rest of section V (Craig, Hodge,
Lewis, Fischer), section VI (counterpossibles), and the second paragraph of
the conclusion; notes cite those only as attributed summary.

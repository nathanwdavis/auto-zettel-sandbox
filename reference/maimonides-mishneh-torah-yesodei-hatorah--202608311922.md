Bibliographic record. Maimonides (1138-1204) is not "Judaism" as such, but
Hilchot Yesodei HaTorah is the classical Jewish statement of what kind of
oneness is being asserted of God, which is the question that actually divides
the traditions. Captured passage: 1:7. Text and Touger translation retrieved
from Sefaria (CC-BY-NC); the CSL entry records the underlying Moznaim print
edition that Sefaria names as the source of the translation.

Bibliographic record. Bruce Ware's paper to the Evangelical Theological
Society's 53rd annual meeting (Colorado Springs, 15 November 2001),
published in the Society's journal 45/2 (June 2002), from the PDF the
Society posts in its open archive; tiered `peer-reviewed` on the venue. The
author note places Ware at The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary. It is
the only work of Ware's on file in his own words: every other Ware statement
in this repository is quoted by someone else (Grudem quoting *God's Lesser
Glory*; Helm and Van Horn quoting *God's Greater Glory*). Its subject is
narrower than the sovereignty-and-freedom question: by its own footnote 7
(not captured) it restricts itself to open theism's denial of exhaustive
divine foreknowledge and does not argue the question of libertarian freedom,
so what it supplies here is Ware's foreknowledge argument, the same argument
Grudem quotes from *God's Lesser Glory*, and Ware's statement of the
inference from libertarian freedom to the denial of foreknowledge.

The article is in copyright (the Society and the author) and served openly
by its publisher, so the capture is bounded excerpts per the repository's
excerpt policy: seven blocks, 2,782 words of 10,461 (27 percent), each a
contiguous run of the PDF extraction. Captured: section I to the paragraph
on what is essential to open theism; sections II.1.f-g (the idols of Isaiah
40-48 and God's self-chosen basis for asserting deity); section II.3.f (the
crucifixion, Sanders and Boyd); sections II.4.b and d (suffering and risk);
the opening of section III (why no council spoke); and section IV. NOT
captured: the rest of section I, the preamble to section II, sections
II.1.a-e and h, II.2, II.3.a-e and g-i, II.4.a and c, and the second comment
of section III quoting Tidball; notes cite those only as attributed summary.

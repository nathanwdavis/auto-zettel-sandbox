Scripture, cited in-text per SBL and excluded from the bibliography (FR-9).
Twelve passages bundled the way the general-revelation and Qur'an captures
are, for the owner's "hidden knowledge of God" inquiry (202609151845). They
fall into three groups: creaturely knowledge as partial and bounded (1 Cor.
13:9-12; Rom. 11:33-34; Isa. 55:8-9; Job 11:7-9; Ps. 139:6); God as the one
who conceals and who reveals (Prov. 25:2; Dan. 2:20-22; Amos 3:7; 1 Cor.
2:9-12); and the New Testament sense of *mysterion* as a thing formerly hidden
and now made known (Col. 1:26-27; Eph. 3:3-5), with John 16:12-13 as the one
passage in which a speaker announces knowledge he is withholding. KJV (public
domain) as served by bible-api.com. Deuteronomy 29:29 has its own reference,
[[deuteronomy-29-29-the-secret-things-and-the-revealed-things--202609151849]].

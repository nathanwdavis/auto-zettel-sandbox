Bibliographic record. Dennis R. Venema's essay review of Stephen C.
Meyer's *Signature in the Cell* in Perspectives on Science and Christian
Faith 62, no. 4 (December 2010): 276-83 — the journal of the American
Scientific Affiliation, a network of evangelical Christians in the
sciences. Venema, then associate professor and chair of biology at
Trinity Western University and a contributor to the BioLogos Foundation,
is the review's author; the piece drew a formal response from Meyer in
PSCF 63, no. 3 (see
[[meyer-of-molecules-and-straw-men--202609011311]]). The pair is the
accessible instance of the BioLogos-versus-Discovery-Institute debate
the INBOX asked a remote-capable run to collect: the
irreducible-complexity-specific BioLogos web series named there is
bot-blocked (HTTP 429) from this environment class, while the ASA
serves both halves of this published exchange openly.

PSCF articles of this vintage carry no DOI, so verification is
capture-based: the journal's own PDF, served openly by the publisher at
the URL above, is excerpted (bounded blocks, roughly 45 percent) in
raw/202609011310-venema-seeking-a-signature-pscf-excerpts.txt. The
skipped sections (the RNA-world detail, the common-ancestry section,
most of "Odds and Ends", endnotes) are cited only as attributed summary
and never quoted.

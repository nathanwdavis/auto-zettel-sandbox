Bibliographic record. The 1859 first edition (London: John Murray), Freeman
bibliographical number F373 — the edition in which the argument of Chapter
III, "Struggle for Existence," appears in its original wording (later
editions rework several of its sentences). Only Chapter III (printed pp.
60-79) is captured; it is the chapter that generalizes Malthus's
geometrical-increase principle to "the whole animal and vegetable kingdoms."

This is the base's primary source on compounding as a universal biological
tendency that is everywhere suppressed: every organic being multiplies
geometrically in potential, no habitat sustains that increase, and the
standing gap between the two is what Darwin names the struggle for
existence. It extends the base's Malthus source (the human collision case
the chapter explicitly builds on) from one species to all of them.

The raw capture holds two textually independent copies of Chapter III: the
Project Gutenberg hand-keyed transcription of the first edition (eBook
#1228, block 1) and the Darwin Online scholarly transcription of F373
(block 2), neither access-restricted, both public domain (1859
publication). Wording on which both blocks agree is treated as verified
1859 first-edition text; page locators in notes are the printed page
numbers in block 2's "[page]" markers. The work predates ISBN and DOI, so
verification rests on the raw capture; publisher, place, and year are
transcribed from the Darwin Online item's own bibliographic header, which
matches the Gutenberg edition statement ("1859, First Edition").

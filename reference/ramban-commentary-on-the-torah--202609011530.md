Bibliographic record. Nachmanides (Ramban, 1194-1270) is the pivotal figure
for this repository's purposes: a halakhist and biblical commentator who was
also a kabbalist, and whose Torah commentary states kabbalistic doctrine
openly enough to cite. `primary-text` because it is used here as a primary
statement of a kabbalistic position, not as scholarship about one.

Two comments captured complete: **Leviticus 18:25**, his "secret" of
Deuteronomy 32:8-9 and 4:19 -- the nations allotted to constellations and to
angelic princes, with the Land of Israel alone held directly by "the Glorious
Name" -- and **Exodus 20:3**, on *al panai* ("before My face"), on divine
jealousy as a thing said of Israel only, and on the three kinds of
idol-worship.

Dating note: the `issued` date 1971 is the Chavel translation used, which is
what the capture reproduces and what a reader would consult; the Hebrew
original is thirteenth-century and is dated in prose wherever the argument
turns on it. The translation is served by Sefaria under CC-BY, which is why it
can be quoted here at length.

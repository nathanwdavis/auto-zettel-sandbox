Bibliographic record. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on rigid
designation, first published 2006-10-24, substantive revision 2022-02-03 (the
`issued` date; `original-date` marks first publication), by Joseph LaPorte
(Hope College). Recorded as reputable-secondary: the entry is an expert survey
of the literature on rigidity, not a primary statement of Kripke's position.

The entry is in copyright (2022, by the author), so the capture is a bounded
excerpt per this repository's excerpt policy, following the precedent set by
`ratzsch-koperski-teleological-arguments-for-gods-existence--202609011003`:
the preamble and section 1 (defining rigidity), and the passage in section 3
that states rigidity's relation to descriptivism. Later sections on the
formal semantics of rigidity, on natural-kind terms, and the bibliography were
NOT captured; notes cite them only as attributed summary and never quote them.

Bibliographic record. Stephen C. Meyer's "Author Exchange" response to
Dennis Venema's essay review of *Signature in the Cell*, in Perspectives
on Science and Christian Faith 63, no. 3 (September 2011): 171-82.
Meyer writes as director of the Discovery Institute's Center for
Science and Culture and, per the article's own biography box, as a
longtime member of the American Scientific Affiliation, whose journal
this is. The piece answers
[[venema-seeking-a-signature--202609011310]] (the historical-connection
link): together the two form an openly published exchange between a
BioLogos-affiliated evangelical biologist and the Discovery Institute's
lead proponent over the information-based design argument.

PSCF articles of this vintage carry no DOI, so verification is
capture-based: the journal's own PDF, served openly by the publisher at
the URL above, is excerpted (bounded blocks, roughly a third) in
raw/202609011311-meyer-of-molecules-and-straw-men-pscf-excerpts.txt.
The skipped sections (the argument-summary opening of "From the Horse's
Mouth", the RNA-world and Yarus/direct-templating sections, endnotes)
are cited only as attributed summary and never quoted.

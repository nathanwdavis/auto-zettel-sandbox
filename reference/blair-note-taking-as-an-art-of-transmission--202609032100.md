Bibliographic record. Peer-reviewed historical scholarship on note-taking as a
practice, which is what this base previously had none of: before this note the
`zettelkasten method` topic rested on Ahrens (an 11-page publisher preview),
Luhmann's own "Kommunikation mit Zettelkaesten", and Schmidt's archivist
account — a how-to, a practitioner, and that practitioner's cataloguer. Blair
is a historian of early modern information management writing in a refereed
journal, and she is independent of the Luhmann line entirely, so she can
corroborate or limit its claims rather than restate them.

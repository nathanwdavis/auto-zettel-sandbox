Bibliographic record. Luhmann's own first-person account of his Zettelkasten —
the primary source behind the method this base's "zettelkasten method" topic
covers; until this note the topic rested on Ahrens's secondary presentation
alone. The complete essay was captured in Manfred Kuehn's English translation
as published at luhmann.surge.sh (translator credited on the page itself); the
German original appeared in the 1981 Noelle-Neumann festschrift cited in
csl_json, whose volume details (editors, publisher, place, year, ISBN) were
confirmed against Open Library (works/OL22696154W, books/OL3061620M) at capture
time. The essay's page range within that volume (commonly cited as 222–228)
was not independently verified, so no page field is recorded. Quotations and
locators in notes built on this reference cite the translation's section
numerals (I–IV), which the capture preserves.

Bibliographic record. The paper that restarted the modern literature on the
testing effect, and the item inquiry 202609032123 named as its sharpest
outstanding lead: if retrieval is what consolidates learning, then handing
memory to paper might have a measurable cost, and Chavigny's position in
[[the-note-became-an-external-memory-only-when-memorizing-came-to-be-seen-as-a-cost--202609032115]]
would be arguing against something. Whether the paper actually delivers that is
worked out in [[retrieval-not-re-exposure-is-what-makes-learning-last--202609040340]]
and [[having-the-notes-to-hand-buys-present-performance-not-durable-knowledge--202609040345]];
the short answer is that it supplies the mechanism and not the measurement.

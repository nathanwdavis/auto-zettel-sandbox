Bibliographic record. Moses Cordovero (RaMaK, Safed, 1522-1570) completed
*Pardes Rimmonim* in 1548; it was first printed at Krakow in 1592, which is
the `issued` date above. The capture is **Gate 4 entire**, all ten chapters --
*Sha'ar Etzem ve-Kelim*, the Gate of Essence and Vessels.

`primary-text` because it is cited here as a kabbalist's own statement of a
kabbalistic doctrine and of the dispute about it, not as scholarship about
someone else's position. Cordovero is a witness to the other two parties in
the ordinary way of a reporter who quotes at length before disagreeing: he
transcribes Menachem Recanati and R. David (*Magen David*) in their own words
across chapters 1-2 and only then argues. Nothing in this repository rests on
his summary being fair to them; where a note reports what Recanati or R. David
held, it reports what Cordovero quotes them as holding, and says so.

`versionSource` http://www.hebrew.grimoar.cz/kordovero/pardes_rimonim.htm,

**No translation exists on this route, and the renderings here are the
repository's own.** Sefaria's API reports Hebrew as the only available
language for this work. Every English phrase quoted from Gate 4 in the notes
built on this reference was rendered by this repository from the Hebrew in the
capture, and is marked as such at the point of use. This base has no

**A limit on the edition.** This is a plain digital text without apparatus,
folio numbers, or variant readings, so locators in this repository are by gate,

rather than by the folio of a printed edition. A later run with access to a
printed *Pardes Rimmonim* could add folio locators; nothing here depends on
them.

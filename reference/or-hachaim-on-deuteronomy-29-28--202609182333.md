The Or HaChaim on the verse, Hebrew (public domain as Sefaria declares it) and
Eliyahu Munk's English (CC-BY as Sefaria declares it; no translation year is
printed, so the access date stands in). One comment: the verse is Israel's
reply to Moses, declining responsibility for sins committed in private and
accepting, "for us and our children forever", the mutual responsibility
(*arvut*) for public ones. Captured for the 2026-09-15 leads entry on
Deuteronomy 29:28.

(Calvin Translation Society, 1845; public domain) as transcribed by the
Christian Classics Ethereal Library; the whole chapter page is captured
verbatim as HTML. The base's other Calvin reference
([[calvin-institutes-of-the-christian-religion--202609011032]]) captures Book I
chapters 3 and 5 in John Allen's translation; this chapter is filed separately,
on the pattern of the several Summa question references, because it is a
different translator and a different host, and quotations from it follow
Beveridge. Read for inquiry 202609151845: sections 1-4, where Calvin fences
curiosity about the decree with Deut. 29:29 and Prov. 25:2.

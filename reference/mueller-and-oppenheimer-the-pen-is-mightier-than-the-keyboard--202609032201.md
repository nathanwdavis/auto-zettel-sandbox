Bibliographic record. The origin of the "ditch the laptop, take notes by hand"
claim, and by a wide margin the most cited experiment on note taking: the 2021
replication counted more than 1,000 citations of it as of July 2020.

Tiered `peer-reviewed` on the strength of the venue (*Psychological Science*,
refereed; received 11 May 2013, accepted 16 January 2014 per the PubMed
record). The tier describes the article, not the depth of this base's access
to it, which is the next paragraph and is the important one.

**This is an abstract-only capture. Nobody here has read this article.** The
ladder was walked on 2026-09-03 and no open copy exists: Unpaywall reports the
DOI closed with no OA location, OpenAlex records `any_repository_has_fulltext`
false with only a SAGE landing page and a PubMed record, and Semantic Scholar
returns `openAccessPdf` status CLOSED. Unlike its replication, whose authors

How the base works around that, and the limit that remains. The specific
numbers this base states about the original study — its Study 1 effect sizes,

represented in this base only as two rows in a forest plot.

Scripture, cited in-text per SBL and excluded from the bibliography (FR-9).
The Nazareth pericope: Jesus "could there do no mighty work" and "marvelled
because of their unbelief" (6:5-6). The Synoptic anchor for the claim that
unbelief limits miracle. King James Version (public domain), captured via
bible-api.com. Matthew's parallel (13:58, "did not many mighty works there") is
on file only as Aquinas quotes it, in
[[summa-theologiae-secunda-secundae-question-178-the-gift-of--202609230037]].

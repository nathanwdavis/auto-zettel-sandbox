Section LXIV of *De servo arbitrio* (1525) in Henry Cole's 1823 translation as
edited by Henry Atherton for the Sovereign Grace Union (1931; Atherton's
preface says Cole's translation is "used with slight alterations from
Vaughan"), as transcribed by the Christian Classics Ethereal Library, which
serves the work one section to a page; this page is captured verbatim as HTML.
The section is Luther's reply to Erasmus's use of Ezekiel 18:23 ("I desire not
the death of a sinner"), and is where he draws the line between God preached
and God hidden, the *Deus praedicatus* and *Deus absconditus* of the later
literature; the two neighbouring sections (LXII-LXIII) set up the passage and
are not captured. The Latin is not on file. Captured for inquiry 202609151845.

Bibliographic record. The Latin Summa Theologiae was written c. 1265-1274
(`original-date` 1274 marks its end); the cited text is the public-domain
English translation by the Fathers of the English Dominican Province
(Benziger Brothers "Second and Revised Edition", 1920, per New Advent's
colophon). The capture is the COMPLETE Question 2 of the First Part --
"The Existence of God", all three articles including the Five Ways -- in
two independent digitizations of the same translation: BLOCK 1
(quotation anchor) is Project Gutenberg eBook #17611; BLOCK 2 is New
Advent's online edition (newadvent.org/summa/1002.htm), which modernizes
the apparatus only ("Reply Obj. N" -> "Reply to Objection N"; the fool
verse cited "Psalm 53:2" against Gutenberg's "Ps. 52:1", two numberings
of the same psalm). The article bodies match word-for-word across both
witnesses. A medieval work in a 1920 translation predates DOI, and the
translation volume's ISBNs postdate it, so capture-based verification is
the documented path. Cite with scholastic locators (ST I, q.2, a.1-3,
with obj./ad as needed); notes quote BLOCK 1's wording.

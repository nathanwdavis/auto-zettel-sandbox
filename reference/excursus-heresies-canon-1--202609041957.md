Bibliographic record. The editorial excursus that follows Canon I in NPNF2-14,
supplying the doctrinal content of each party the canon names; Percival quotes
William Bright throughout. Public domain.

Recorded as primary-text for the volume it belongs to, but the excursus is the
*editor's* commentary, written in 1900, not a conciliar document. Notes citing
it must say so: it is evidence of what a standard nineteenth-century edition
took the party names to mean, not of what the council said.

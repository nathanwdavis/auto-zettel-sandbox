Bibliographic record. The Social Sciences Replication Project's per-study
replication report for Karpicke & Blunt (2011), authored by Nick Buttrick, Anup
Gampa, Lilian Hummer and Brian Nosek. This is a **distinct source** from the
project's summary Letter in *Nature Human Behaviour* (reference
[[camerer-et-al-evaluating-the-replicability-of-social-science-experiments--202609041855]]):
the Letter reports the aggregate across all 21 studies and prints no per-study
statistics, while this report is the document that states which Karpicke & Blunt
result was targeted and the numbers behind its replication. It is the evidence
the summary summarises.

Bibliographic record. Magisterial Roman Catholic teaching, not evangelical, and
included deliberately: it is the clearest official Christian statement on the
affirmative side of the same-God question, and much of the evangelical
argument is framed against it. Section 3 (on Muslims) is captured; the rest of
the declaration was not.

Bibliographic record. Joseph Pohle's article "Molinism" in volume 10 of *The
Catholic Encyclopedia* (New York: Robert Appleton Company, 1911), read in New
Advent's transcription, which prints the volume, year and a nihil obstat of
1 October 1911 in its citation block. The 1907-1912 encyclopedia is public
domain, so the capture is the complete article; New Advent's own site
copyright covers the website, not the 1911 text, and its navigation and
donation lines are stripped.

Recorded as reputable-secondary: an expert survey written for a reference
work, not a primary statement of any party's position. Two features of the
source bear on how its claims are weighed. It is a century old and predates
the analytic revival of middle knowledge, so it says nothing about
counterfactuals, possible worlds or the grounding objection. And it is
written from inside the Catholic dispute, by a dogmatic theologian, so it
states both the Thomist and the Molinist case in the vocabulary of efficacious
grace and with the Church's dogmatic constraints in view; where it reports
what "the Thomists" hold, that report is a Catholic secondary source's, and
no Thomist primary text (Banez) is on file here.

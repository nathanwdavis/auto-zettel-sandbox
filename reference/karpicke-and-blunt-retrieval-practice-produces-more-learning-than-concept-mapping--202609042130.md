Bibliographic record. The *Science* Report that put retrieval practice head to
head with elaborative concept mapping — the paper the base had, until this
cycle, only known second-hand through the Social Sciences Replication Project's
enumerated lists (reference
[[camerer-et-al-evaluating-the-replicability-of-social-science-experiments--202609041855]]).
It is study 25 of the SSRP's 21, and the paper inquiry
`what-does-karpicke-and-blunt-2011-actually-report-which-of--202609041906` was
filed to identify. This record closes the identification: it is a
retrieval-practice paper, and it is by the same Karpicke who anchors this base's
memory-and-learning cluster (reference 202609040305, 202609040325).

**Excerpt-only capture.** The article PDF carries the AAAS notice "This copy is
for your personal, non-commercial use only" and "Copyright 2011 by the American
Association for the Advancement of Science; all rights reserved." The article
was read in full; only the passages the notes use are committed to `raw/`, with
the SHA-256 of the exact file. This is the same excerpt treatment the base gives
Rowland 2014 and the Karpicke 2025 chapter, and it differs from the full-text
captures of Roediger & Karpicke 2006 and the Agarwal papers, which were
author-posted or open access.

**Access and provenance.** The house ladder (`skills/source-access-triage`) was
climbed in order. (a) science.org is paywalled and was not fetched for text.
(b) Unpaywall reports the DOI as OA, but its one green location
(`americanae.aecid.es`, labelled submittedVersion) is a bibliographic landing
page with a null PDF link — no retrievable text. (c) **worked**: the Purdue
Cognition and Learning Laboratory publications index — the lab of the first
author, Jeffrey D. Karpicke — serves the article PDF directly
(`learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2011/2011_Karpicke_Blunt_Science.pdf`).
That is an author page, rung (c), not a mirror; the same lab index supplied this
base's Roediger & Karpicke 2006 full text. Crossref confirms the record (Science
331(6018):772-775, AAAS, 2011-02-11) and PubMed (PMID 21252317) independently
confirms the abstract wording.

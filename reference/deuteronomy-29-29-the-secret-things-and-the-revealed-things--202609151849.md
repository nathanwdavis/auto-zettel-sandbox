Scripture, cited in-text per SBL and excluded from the bibliography (FR-9).
The one verse on which the Christian secret/revealed distinction rests, and
the anchor text of the owner's "hidden knowledge of God" note. Captured in
three witnesses: the Hebrew (Miqra according to the Masorah, CC-BY-SA, via the
Sefaria API; Hebrew numbering 29:28), the King James Version (public domain,
via bible-api.com), and the JPS Tanakh Gender-Sensitive Edition (CC-BY-NC, via
Sefaria), the last kept because it renders *ha-nistarot* and *ha-niglot* as
"concealed acts" and "overt acts" -- a translation choice that is itself
evidence for the Jewish reading recorded in
[[rashi-on-deuteronomy-29-28-the-secret-things-and-the--202609151851]].
Until this capture the verse was on file only as Grudem quotes it in the ESV
(see [[deut-29-29-partitions-knowledge-and-grudem-partitions-will--202609081906]]).

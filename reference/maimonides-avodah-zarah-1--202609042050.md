Bibliographic record. Maimonides' account of how idolatry began: not as a denial
of God but as a reasoned inference about how He is best honoured. Sefaria titles
these laws "Foreign Worship and Customs of the Nations"; the traditional name is
Hilchot Avodah Zarah.

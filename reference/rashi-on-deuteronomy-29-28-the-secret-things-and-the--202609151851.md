Rashi's comment on the verse English Bibles number Deuteronomy 29:29, captured
in Hebrew and in the Rosenbaum-Silbermann English (both declared public domain
by Sefaria, which serves them). One comment, and the classical Jewish reading
of the verse: the "secret things" are the hidden sins of individuals, which God
alone punishes, and the "revealed things" are open sins, which the community is
bound to judge; the extraordinary points over "to us and to our children" are
read as limiting even that liability to the time after the covenant at Gerizim
and Ebal made Israel "responsible for one another". The comment cites Sanhedrin
43b and Sotah 37b, neither captured here. Captured for inquiry 202609151845
because the base's only prior reading of the verse was Grudem's epistemic one.

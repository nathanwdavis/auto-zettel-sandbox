Bibliographic record. The 1559 Latin edition in John Allen's public-domain
English translation (first published 1813; the Project Gutenberg etext #45001
is the sixth American edition, vol. 1 of 2). Captured verbatim: Book I ch. III
("The Human Mind Naturally Endued With The Knowledge Of God" -- the sensus
divinitatis chapter) and Book I ch. V ("The Knowledge Of God Conspicuous In
The Formation And Continual Government Of The World"), plus ch. VI's title
line for the transition it records (Scripture necessary as guide). The
better-known Beveridge translation words these passages differently; quotes in
this repository follow Allen as captured. `original-date` 1559 marks the
final Latin edition; `issued` is the translation's.

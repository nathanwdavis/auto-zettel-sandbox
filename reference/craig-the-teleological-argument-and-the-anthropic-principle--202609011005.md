Bibliographic record. William Lane Craig's scholarly essay on the
fine-tuning ("anthropic coincidences") form of the teleological argument,
from an edited academic volume (Mellen, 1990), captured from the full text
that Craig's own organization publishes openly on reasonablefaith.org
("Used by permission" appears on the page). The capture is bounded
excerpts per the excerpt policy for in-copyright works: the
Summary/Introduction/physics-examples opening and "The Hypothesis of
Divine Design" + "Concluding Remarks" body; the middle sections
(Classical Cosmology, Biochemistry, the Anthropic Principle
exposition/critique, WAP and a World Ensemble) and the endnotes were NOT
captured -- notes cite them only as attributed summary and never quote
them. The page's own text misprints "World Ensemble" as "Word Ensemble"
once in the Concluding Remarks; the capture preserves the misprint and
notes must not quote that sentence without flagging it.

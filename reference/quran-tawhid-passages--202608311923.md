Scripture, cited in-text per SBL and excluded from the bibliography (FR-9).
Pickthall's 1930 translation is used because it is in the public domain and so
could be captured in full; it is a translation, and the doctrinal weight in
Islam rests on the Arabic. Surah 112 (al-Ikhlas) is captured complete; 4:171
and 5:73 are captured complete as single ayat. Together they give both the
positive statement of tawhid and the explicit Qur'anic rejection of trinitarian
language.

**2026-09-03: a second capture, and why it was needed.** The original capture
was assembled to bear on the same-God question, and all three of its passages
mark difference: divine oneness, "say not 'Three'", and "They surely disbelieve
who say: Lo! Allah is the third of three". The Qur'an also addresses the People of
the Scripture in the opposite direction, and none of those passages were here.
`raw/202609032255-quran-co-reference-passages.txt` adds 2:139, 3:64 and 29:46
complete, in the same Pickthall edition from the same API -- among them "our
Allah and your Allah is One, and unto Him we surrender" (29:46), which is the
most directly on-point verse in the Qur'an for this question.

This is recorded as a defect in the first capture's *selection*, not in its
accuracy: every passage in it is complete and correctly transcribed, and
nothing in it is withdrawn. But a capture that holds the three rejecting
verses and omits the affirming ones is selection-shaped whether or not it was
meant that way, and notes resting on it inherited that shape. `raw/` is
immutable, so the fix is this second capture rather than an edit to the first.
The defect was found by the 2026-09-03 gap-filling graph walk and is the
concrete half of inquiry 202609032122.

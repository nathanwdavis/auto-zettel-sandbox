**Why this source beside al-Tusi rather than instead of him.** Al-Tabrisi writes
a century after al-Tusi and works from him, so as a witness to the *Imami
position* he mostly corroborates. What he adds is something al-Tusi does not
supply and the whole Zaydi half of this cycle's question needed: an
**attribution**. Where al-Tusi says only `عندنا`, "in our view", al-Tabrisi lays
out the field of positions, names who holds each, and attributes the strict
reading to `ابن عمر وبعض الزيدية` — Ibn 'Umar and *some of the Zaydis* — before
claiming it as `مذهبنا`, our school's. That phrase, `بعض الزيدية`, is the same
phrase the modern al-Sabuni uses, eight centuries earlier and in a Shi'i mouth,
which is what makes it possible to say whether the attribution this base was
resting on a single modern author for is old or new.

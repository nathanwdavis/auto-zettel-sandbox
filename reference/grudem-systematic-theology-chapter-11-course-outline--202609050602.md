Unattributed lesson outline accompanying Wayne Grudem's Systematic Theology lectures, presented on waynegrudem.com without byline attribution (site-printed date 2006/03/26 beside 20060326WGrudem.mp3). The PDF file carries document metadata listing Mike Langley as author and 2009-03-18 as creation date (batch re-save). Not established as Grudem's own composition. The outline's page numbers, section headings, and definition wording have been verified against the 1994 edition of Systematic Theology through independent secondary witnesses (Stu Station blog on pages 160, 163, 168, 177; Reasonable Faith article on page 168 for Eternity only) and match exactly except for omnipresence, where Stu Station prints "at every point IN space" while the outline prints "at every point OF space"; establishing this outline as a reliable independent witness to the 1st edition's structure and content, though not as an authored source itself.

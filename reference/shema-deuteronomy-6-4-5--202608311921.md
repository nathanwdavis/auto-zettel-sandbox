Scripture, cited in-text per SBL and excluded from the bibliography (FR-9).
The capture holds the Masoretic Hebrew alongside the JPS gender-sensitive
English. Note the translation crux recorded in the capture: JPS renders 4b
"the ETERNAL alone" following Rashbam and Ibn Ezra, and flags the contrasting
traditional rendering "the LORD is one." The ambiguity is in the Hebrew
(*YHWH echad*), not introduced by the translator, and it matters here because
one rendering asserts exclusivity of allegiance and the other asserts
numerical unity.

Scripture, cited in-text per SBL and excluded from the bibliography (FR-9).
The two classic general-revelation proof texts, bundled like the Qur'an
tawhid passages: Paul's claim that "the invisible things of him from the
creation of the world are clearly seen ... even his eternal power and
Godhead; so that they are without excuse", and the Psalm's "The heavens
declare the glory of God". KJV (public domain) as served by bible-api.com.
In the captured excerpts Calvin cites both passages (Rom. 1:20 quoted, Ps. 19
alluded to as the Psalmist's "language universally known"); Hodge cites
Romans 1 (his other ch. IV proof texts are Ps. 76:1 and Isa. 11:9). That
standing in both authors is why the passages are captured in their own right.

Bibliographic record. The creed promulgated by the Council of Nicaea in 325,
in the English of the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers second series (1900),
edited by Philip Schaff with Henry R. Percival for this volume. Public domain;
the capture is the whole page, council text and editorial notes together.

This is the text of 325, not the creed of 381 recited in churches today. It
carries the anathema clause, which the 381 creed drops.

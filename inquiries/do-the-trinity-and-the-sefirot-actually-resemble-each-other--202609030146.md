Second gap from the 2026-09-03 graph query. The base holds that the internal-distinction axis fails to sort Judaism from Christianity, because Kabbalah answers yes to it (internal-plurality-is-not-what-divides-the-monotheisms--202609011543, tagged contested). But no note compares the two articulations ON THE MERITS -- three persons versus ten sefirot, consubstantial persons versus emanations, whether the sefirot are God's essence (atzmut) or instruments (kelim), and whether either tradition's distinctions are personal. Note 202609011543 itself flags the comparison as this repository's own inference; note 202609011811 warns that from 'both traditions articulate God internally' nothing follows about whether the articulations are the same. Needs scholarly comparative sources, not the primary texts already captured; Scholem, Idel and Wolfson are the obvious names and are all in copyright, so check what is actually capturable before promising a comparison.

**Progress (2026-09-03), not an answer.** This cycle established one thing
bearing on the question and failed to find the sources needed to settle it.

Established: the structural parallel is not only a Christian apologetic claim.
Leon Modena, a seventeenth-century Jewish critic of Kabbalah, reached the same
judgement from the opposite motive --- that the sefirot point to "a plurality
within God similar to the Christian doctrine of the Trinity" --- and treated it
as disqualifying (see
the-first-jewish-answer-to-christian-kabbalism-conceded-the-parallel--202609030155).
So the base now has the resemblance asserted by a hostile Jewish witness as
well as by a Christian apologist, which is corroboration that a resemblance was
perceived, from two directions with nothing in common but the perception.

NOT established, and this is why the inquiry stays open: none of that is a
comparison ON THE MERITS. Nobody in the base compares three persons with ten
sefirot, consubstantiality with emanation, or asks whether the sefirot are
God's essence (*atzmut*) or His instruments (*kelim*) --- the distinction that
note 202609011543 says would narrow the contrast sharply, and which no captured
source addresses. A perceived resemblance reported by two parties who both had
a use for it is weak evidence about the doctrines themselves.

What was tried and failed: a search for open-access comparative scholarship
returned only blogs, forums and encyclopaedia summaries --- `general-web` tier,
below what a claim this contested needs, and `lint_citations` would in any case
require three distinct verified sources for a note tagged `contested`. The
standard scholarship (Scholem, Idel, Wolfson) is in copyright and was not
reachable this cycle. One concrete lead did surface and is filed in INBOX:
Abraham Abulafia is reported to have attacked the sefirot in the thirteenth
century as no better than the Trinity, which would put a KABBALIST on the
record about the parallel four centuries before Modena --- but that report came
from a non-citable source and needs grounding in Idel's Abulafia scholarship
before any note may use it.



**Answer (2026-09-04). No, not at the points the question names -- and the
comparison was made from primary texts on both sides rather than from the
comparative scholarship this entry asked for.**

The thing this entry said was missing has been captured. Moses Cordovero's
*Pardes Rimmonim* devotes its whole fourth gate, *Sha'ar Etzem ve-Kelim*, to
exactly the *atzmut*-or-*kelim* question, quoting both parties at length before
answering: Menachem Recanati and R. Yehudah Hayyat for the instruments,
R. David of *Magen David* for the essence. The gate is captured entire, in
Hebrew, public domain, at reference 202609041700. The Christian side was given
matching standing by a second capture on the existing Hodge reference
(202609011033), Part I ch. VI §§ 3-5: the accounts of Sabellianism and
Arianism, the Nicene creed with its anathemas, and what the councils decided.

Four findings, in the order they bear on the question.

1. **The question this entry named is a real dispute inside Kabbalah, not a
   modern reconstruction** -- Cordovero opens by calling it "a great confusion
   fallen among the later kabbalists"
   ([[whether-the-sefirot-are-gods-essence-or-his-instruments-is-a-dispute-inside-kabbalah--202609041720]]).
2. **Kabbalah's most influential answer refuses the choice**: ten inner sefirot
   that are essence, clothed in ten outer sefirot that are vessels
   ([[cordovero-settles-the-essence-vessel-question-by-doubling-the-sefirot--202609041725]]).
3. **The two options Kabbalah left open are the two the councils shut** --
   aspects relative to God's actions is Sabellianism's shape, instruments of a
   different essence is Arianism's
   ([[the-two-kabbalistic-readings-of-the-sefirot-are-the-two-the-councils-ruled-out--202609041730]]),
   and the traditions also differ in how they hold such a question at all
   ([[a-tradition-that-defines-its-articulation-and-one-that-keeps-arguing-it--202609041740]]).
4. **The deepest divergence is not plurality but exhaustiveness**: the sefirot
   have a One outside them who is not one of them, and the persons have no
   fourth
   ([[the-sefirot-have-a-term-outside-them-and-the-persons-have-none--202609041745]]).

**What is still not established, and why this is marked answered anyway.**
Scholem, Idel and Wolfson remain unreached, so the alignment in findings 3 and
4 is this repository's own inference from primary texts, and the two notes
carrying it are tagged `contested` and say so in their own bodies. That is a
real limit, not a formality: a comparative scholar might place the sefirot
against the Trinity differently, and if one is ever captured saying so, these
notes must be re-scored rather than defended. The entry is closed because the
base can now say something substantive and grounded in answer to the question,
which it could not on 2026-09-03; the hunt for the comparative scholarship is
kept alive as its own INBOX entry rather than by leaving this one open
indefinitely.

Filed by the user (2026-09-01) for a future run. Survey and rank the strongest design-type (teleological) arguments as they function within evangelical natural theology -- candidates to evaluate include Paley-style biological design, fine-tuning of physical constants, cosmic teleology/order arguments, irreducible-complexity claims, and beauty/intelligibility arguments -- grounding each in named proponents and verified sources, and noting evangelical assessments (which arguments evangelical philosophers and theologians actually endorse or reject, and why). Connects to the existing Abrahamic-conceptions cluster (see moc/abrahamic-conceptions-of-god--202608311945) and to the Anselm cycle's distinction between argument-fixed reference and tradition identification (permanent/anselms-formula-identifies-god-by-description-alone--202609010840).

**Answer (2026-09-01):** Ranked as the arguments function in evangelical
natural theology today, on the sources captured this cycle (Paley's text,
the SEP survey, and Craig's programmatic essay -- see the caveat at the
end).

1. **Cosmic fine-tuning.** The lead argument. The "anthropic coincidences"
   -- constants and initial conditions where "Even an extraordinarily
   small change ... would have made life impossible anywhere in the
   universe" (SEP 4.1) -- have "breathed new life into the teleological
   argument" (Craig). Its evangelical standing rests on two features:
   the data are mainstream physics, and the argument sits upstream of
   Darwinian selection, which answered Paley but cannot touch the
   constants. Named proponents: Craig (captured endorsement), the
   Tennant "wider teleology" lineage he cites, Robin Collins (named in
   SEP). See
   [[craig-restakes-teleology-on-anthropic-fine-tuning--202609011012]],
   [[the-design-arguments-center-of-gravity-moved-from-organisms-to-physics--202609011015]].

2. **Paley-style biological design.** The historical flagship and still
   the paradigm of the design intuition: contrivance -- parts "framed and
   put together for a purpose" -- read directly off organisms, with "the
   same proof that the eye was made for vision as ... that the telescope
   was made for assisting it." Ranked second because evangelicals
   themselves largely grant the standard verdict -- Craig opens by
   conceding it is "Widely thought to have been demolished by Hume and
   Darwin" -- and now deploy its logic through arguments 1 and 3 rather
   than on Paley's original ground. See
   [[paley-reads-contrivance-off-the-watch-and-transfers-it-to-nature--202609011010]].

3. **Irreducible/specified complexity (Intelligent Design).** The
   contemporary biological revival: Behe's irreducible complexity and
   Dembski's specified complex information (SEP 4.2). Endorsed by a
   significant evangelical constituency, but ranked below fine-tuning
   because it is contested even among believing scientists and the SEP
   records the "vociferous criticism and opposition" it drew, including
   the charge that it argues from gaps. (Direct captures of evangelical
   critics and defenders are still lacking -- see INBOX.)

4. **Order/lawfulness arguments (the Fifth Way lineage).** From the
   ordered regularity of nature as such rather than any particular
   contrivance: SEP notes the design argument "goes back at least to the
   Greeks and in extremely clipped form comprises one of Aquinas's Five
   Ways." In evangelical use it typically appears as a frame for
   argument 1 (why are there elegant laws at all?) rather than as a
   free-standing proof; no primary Aquinas capture yet.

5. **Beauty and intelligibility.** The intuition SEP places at the root
   of the whole family -- that accident "couldn't produce the order,
   beauty, elegance, and seeming purpose that we experience" (SEP 1) --
   promoted to an argument in its own right. Ranked last because in the
   captured sources it functions as the shared intuition the other four
   formalize, not yet as a developed inference; SEP's conclusion still
   credits "the incredible intricacy and the beauty of things in nature"
   with real persuasive force.

Two structural results worth keeping regardless of ranking: the arguments'
center of gravity has moved from organisms to physics
([[the-design-arguments-center-of-gravity-moved-from-organisms-to-physics--202609011015]]),
and every argument in the family concludes to "an artificer or
artificers" -- a maker, not provably one maker -- so identifying the
designer with the God evangelicals worship is a separate step, exactly
parallel to the step Anselm takes in prayer
([[the-design-argument-concludes-to-a-maker-not-to-one-maker--202609011016]];
this connects the inquiry to the Abrahamic-conceptions cluster as the
filing anticipated).

**Caveat:** the ranking's evangelical-assessment evidence is currently one
prominent evangelical philosopher (Craig 1990) plus the SEP's field
survey. A follow-up INBOX entry asks a future run to capture a broader
base of named evangelical assessments (e.g. Plantinga, McGrath, a
BioLogos-vs-Discovery-Institute exchange on ID) and to revisit ranks 3-5
against them.

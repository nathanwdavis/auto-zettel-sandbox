What does Molina's doctrine of middle knowledge (scientia media) claim about the relation of God's sovereign will to human free will, as stated in Molina's own Concordia (1588) and in the IEP article 'Middle Knowledge', and how does it bear on the compatibilist position this base holds from Grudem's chapter 16?

**Answered 2026-09-09** (ad-hoc session on branch claude/trusting-einstein-v2es9x, session as agent). Captured: Molina, *Concordia* (Paris 1876 ed., archive.org), disp. 52 pp. 317-318 and 323-324 and disp. 53 p. 337, transcribed from the page images (committed) and checked against two OCR witnesses; the IEP entry "Middle Knowledge" (Laing), as bounded excerpts, 46 percent of the text; and the 1911 and 1908 Catholic Encyclopedia articles "Molinism" (Pohle) and "Congregatio de Auxiliis" (Astrain), complete. Four references, four literature notes, seven permanent notes (the result notes above), three amendments (202609061923, 202609081830, 202609081914), and a new MOC section "Middle knowledge, added 2026-09-09". What remains unread: Freddoso's translation, Banez and any Thomist primary text, the modern Molinist literature and its critics, Molina's disputations 47-48 and 31.

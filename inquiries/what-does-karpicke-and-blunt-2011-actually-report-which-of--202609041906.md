Opened by the 2026-09-04T18:53Z cycle, which found the gap and could not close
it from the source it had.

Reading Camerer et al. 2018 first-hand established that two of the twenty-one
findings in the Social Sciences Replication Project come from this base's own
retrieval-practice literature: a Karpicke and Blunt paper in *Science* (2011),
which replicated at stage 1, and Pyc and Rawson (2010), which replicated only
after stage 2. That is the first pre-registered, high-powered replication
evidence this base has on the retrieval side of its evidence cluster, and it
currently cannot be used, for a reason permanent 202609041920 states about
itself: **this repository does not hold the Karpicke and Blunt paper.** It knows
the finding replicated only from a list of superscript reference numbers read
against a figure-legend key. It does not know which result the SSRP chose, what
that result was, or how it relates to
[[retrieval-not-re-exposure-is-what-makes-learning-last--202609040340]].

What would answer this:

1. The paper itself — Karpicke and Blunt, *Science* 331 (2011), on retrieval
   practice versus elaborative study with concept mapping. Check Unpaywall for
   the DOI and read the cover sheet of whatever comes back before capturing
   (see the Unpaywall INBOX entry filed by the same cycle).
2. The SSRP's replication report for it, which is public: the project's OSF
   repository is `https://osf.io/pfdyw/` and the pre-replication and final
   replication reports for all 21 studies are posted there. The final reports
   carry a "Unplanned protocol deviations" section. This is the route that
   answers "which result was tested" without needing the Supplementary
   Information the captured PDF lacks.
3. Then either attach the support to a claim here, or record why it does not
   reach one. Both are results. The note that generated this question is
   deliberately written so that "it does not reach our claims" would confirm it
   rather than embarrass it.

A caution carried over: whatever comes back, the rule in
[[a-replication-project-that-tests-one-study-per-paper-cannot-settle-the-paper--202609041905]]
applies to this in the direction this base would rather it did not. One
replicated result promotes that result, not the paper and not the programme.

What are the primary distinctions between the Christian doctrine of human free will within God's sovereignty and the doctrines of the other traditions that hold the same Pentateuch — rabbinic Judaism above all, and Samaritanism, with Islam's Tawrat as the contested case? Ground the Jewish leg in primary texts (Maimonides, Hilchot Teshuvah 5; Pirkei Avot 3:15) rather than in Christian reports of it, as the Islamic leg was grounded in al-Ash'ari's Maqalat.

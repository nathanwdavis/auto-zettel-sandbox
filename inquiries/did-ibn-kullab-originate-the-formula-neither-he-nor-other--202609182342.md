Promoted from fleeting note 202609051950 (2026-09-05, narrowed 2026-09-07) by the 2026-09-18 fleeting sweep. The attribution of the formula to Ibn Kullab as al-Ash'ari reports him is grounded in the Maqalat (reference maqalat-al-islamiyyin-wa-ikhtilaf-al-musallin--202609072129, permanent al-ash-ari-s-maqalat-puts-the-islamic-attributes-at-the--202609072135); the origination question is not, because the Maqalat's 'sharh qawl' frame reports a doctrine rather than a chain of transmission. al-Shahrastani's al-Milal wa'l-Nihal and Ibn Kullab's reported works are the route.

"Worship" is the base's most-used and least-examined word: it appears in some
sixty notes and is defined in none. Every major thread leans on a separation the
base has never cashed out.

- `bogardus-and-urban-make-co-reference-turn-on-dossier-dominance--202609010838`
  closes by "separating worship from reference -- there may be conditions on
  worship beyond referring to the right being".
- `shituf-grades-the-worshipper-instead-of-identifying-the-worshipped--202609011542`
  and `kabbalah-separates-acknowledging-god-from-addressing-him--202609011540`
  both turn on worship being a different act from identifying.
- Tachin relocates the whole question onto "the real substance of worship and
  its approval by God" (`the-same-god-question-turns-on-reference-versus-description--202608311942`).
- `the-classical-summons-presupposes-a-shared-acknowledgement-and-disputes-the-worship--202609041215`
  makes the classical tafsir position turn on exactly this split: shared
  acknowledgement, disputed worship.

So four independent traditions and one analytic paper all park the residue in
"worship", and the base has no note saying what is in it. Ask what conditions
each tradition imposes -- intention, addressee, rite, mediation, exclusivity --
and whether they are conditions on the *act* or on its *acceptance*. If the
distinction is act-versus-acceptance, several apparent disagreements in the base
may be agreements about reference and disagreements about approval.

The base is deep on Christianity-and-Islam (the whole tafsir and fiqh sweep) and
deep on Christianity-and-Kabbalah (`christian-kabbalism-claimed-kabbalah-compels-the-trinity--202609011811`
through `the-sefirot-have-a-term-outside-them-and-the-persons-have-none--202609041745`).
The third leg is empty. `shituf-grades-the-worshipper-instead-of-identifying-the-worshipped--202609011542`
and the Rema gloss are worked only in the Christian direction, and a query for
"idolatry avodah zarah Noahide law" reports that no note uses the term `noahide`.

The gap hides a known asymmetry: on the standard reading Maimonides holds
Muslims to be strict monotheists whose worship is not avodah zarah, while
treating Christian worship as idolatrous -- an ordering exactly opposite to the
Christian sources the base has collected, where Islam is the harder case. The
base already has Maimonides on non-composition
(`maimonides-denies-any-composition-in-gods-unity--202608311932`) but nothing on
his verdict about either neighbour.

From primary sources (Mishneh Torah, Hilchot Avodat Kochavim and Hilchot Ma'achalot Asurot;
the Responsum to Obadiah the Proselyte; Meiri on the "nations bound by religion";
the Noahide-law material, and the shituf discussion in the Tosafot the Rema
draws on): does the shituf category apply to Islam at all, and if it applies to
neither Islam nor Judaism's own reading of itself, is shituf a Christianity-shaped
category rather than a general one -- which would sharpen
`goshen-gottstein-calls-shituf-a-good-internal-category-and-a-poor-description`.

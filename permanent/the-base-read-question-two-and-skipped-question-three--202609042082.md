This repository read *Summa Theologiae* I q.2 for the Five Ways and stopped
there. The survey names what that skipped: "The centrality of DDS to medieval
philosophical theology is shown by its position in the order of topics in
Aquinas' *Summa Theologica*. It occurs as Question 3 right after Question 2 on
the existence of God."

The omission has a shape, and it is the same shape as a result the base reached
by another route. [[the-design-argument-concludes-to-a-maker-not-to-one-maker--202609011016]]
found that arguments from the world establish *that* a maker exists and do not
by themselves establish which being, or that there is only one. Question 3 is
where Aquinas addresses exactly that deficit: having argued that God exists, he
asks what sort of thing has been shown to exist, and answers by exclusion —
not a body, not matter and form, not essence plus existence, not subject plus
accident.

So the two halves the base has been treating as separate topics are one
argument in its source. The Fifth Way infers a director from regularity (see
[[the-fifth-way-infers-a-director-from-regularity-not-contrivance--202609011211]]);
question 3 is what stops that director from being one more composite item in the
world alongside the things it directs — since a composite "is posterior to its
component parts, and is dependent on them."

Filed as a claim about this base's reading, not about Aquinas: a natural
theology cluster built out of question 2 and a same-God cluster built out of
doctrines of God were never connected, and the connection was one question
further down the page in a text already on file.

When every participant in a bounded space carries a compounding growth
tendency, the binding constraint stops merely limiting growth and starts
*choosing* among the growers: since all cannot realize their exponent,
which ones do is decided by differential success under the checks, and
suppression itself becomes a sorting mechanism. Darwin's Chapter III is
the primary case
([[darwin-on-the-origin-of-species--202609010908]]): every organic being
without exception multiplies geometrically in potential — his floor case
is the slowest breeder, not the fastest — while no country can support
the product, so destruction is continuous and the survivors are the ones
that pressed hardest or fit best; his wedge image makes the mechanism
explicit, a surface already packed, where one wedge enters only by
forcing others out
([[darwin-applies-malthus-with-manifold-force-to-all-life--202609010909]]).

This elaborates the base's Malthus note
([[no-linear-supply-can-permanently-outpace-a-compounding-demand--202609010704]])
by asking what happens *after* its conclusion: there, one compounding
demand meets a linear supply and some check must fall on the compounder;
here, Darwin universalizes the compounding side — the check is guaranteed
to fall somewhere, and its distribution across many simultaneous
compounders is what does the selecting. The engine is still reinvestment
([[reinvested-returns-compound--202701010011]]): offspring beget
offspring, which is exactly why suppressed lines lose ground
exponentially rather than linearly. And it inverts
[[long-horizon-compounding-is-bounded-by-institutional-survival--202609010509]]:
there a bound (institutional death) is the failure mode that ends one
compounder's run; here bounds are the permanent operating condition that
makes the population of compounders evolve. Stating the mechanism as a
domain-general law — that any arena of many compounders under a shared
bound (firms in a market, ideas competing for attention) behaves
selectively — is this note's inference, not Darwin's claim; his claim
covers organic beings, and his particular checks stay with him.

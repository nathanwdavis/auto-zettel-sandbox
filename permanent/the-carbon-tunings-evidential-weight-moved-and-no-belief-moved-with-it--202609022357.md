There is a test that separates a belief's ground from its confirmation without
asking anyone to introspect: watch what happens to the belief when the evidence
is revalued. A ground is load-bearing, so a belief resting on one has to track
its strength. A confirmation is an increment, so a belief that merely receives
one does not.

The carbon resonance has been revalued, repeatedly and in public. Hoyle's own
reading treated the coincidence of levels as decisive. Livio et al. found that
"a ~= 60 keV change" in the Hoyle state energy "could be tolerated, and thus
the amount of fine-tuning required was not as severe as first believed."
Oberhummer et al. tightened it again to "a narrow window of ~= 0.5% around the
observed strong force". Then the lattice-QCD recomputation loosened it once
more, concluding that "the formation of carbon and oxygen in our Universe would
survive a change of ~= 2% in mq or ~= 2% in alpha_em" (see
[[epelbaum-viability-of-carbon-based-life--202609022348]], pp. 1, 3-4, and
[[the-lattice-recomputation-loosened-the-hoyle-states-tuning--202609022352]]).
Most sharply, the same authors record that their error bars are "compatible
with a vanishing d(epsilon)/d(M_pi) (complete lack of fine-tuning)" — the best
current calculation does not exclude there being no tuning here at all.

A theistic belief grounded on this evidence would have had to weaken in 1989,
strengthen in 2001, weaken again in 2013, and hold its verdict hostage to the
next lattice determination. No such belief is on record doing that. The
believers who cite fine-tuning went on believing at constant strength while the
number underneath it moved by a factor of several and acquired an uncertainty
that reaches zero.

That is not the incoherence it can be made to look like. It is what confirming
without grounding predicts: an increment that varies leaves the level it was
added to roughly where it was, and the argument was issued as an increment in
the first place (see
[[a-confirming-use-of-fine-tuning-states-its-own-non-coerciveness--202609022356]]).
The honest cost is that the same insensitivity is what an unfalsifiable
commitment would also produce, and this note does not by itself tell the two
apart — what distinguishes them is whether the believer can say where the
belief does rest, and whether that ground is itself answerable to anything.

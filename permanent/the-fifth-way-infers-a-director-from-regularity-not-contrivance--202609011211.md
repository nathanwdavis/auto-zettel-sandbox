Aquinas's fifth way and Paley's watch are usually filed together, but
their data differ in kind. Paley's datum is contrivance -- an intricate
arrangement of parts that visibly serves a purpose, read off a
particular object like the watch or the eye. Aquinas's
datum is lawlike regularity in things that cannot intend it: unintelligent
natural bodies "act for an end, and this is evident from their acting
always, or nearly always, in the same way, so as to obtain the best
result", so "not fortuitously, but designedly, do they achieve their end";
what lacks intelligence must "be directed by some being endowed with
knowledge and intelligence; as the arrow is shot to its mark by the
archer" (see [[aquinas-summa-theologiae-i-q2--202609011201]], ST I, q.2,
a.3). The premise needs no intricate mechanism -- any reliable
goal-tracking by the mindless will do -- which is why the SEP survey can
say the design argument "in extremely clipped form comprises one of
Aquinas's Five Ways" while sorting the two under different inference
patterns (see
[[ratzsch-koperski-teleological-arguments-for-gods-existence--202609011003]]).

That difference in datum is a difference in exposure. A selection story
of Darwin's kind explains contrivance in organisms and so presses on
Paley directly; the fifth way's regularity claim sits a level lower --
at the lawfulness selection itself presupposes -- which is this note's
own way of putting why the argument's center of gravity could migrate
from organisms to physics and leave something recognizably Thomist
standing (see
[[the-design-arguments-center-of-gravity-moved-from-organisms-to-physics--202609011015]]).
And the fifth way wears on its sleeve the gap the whole family shares:
its conclusion is "some intelligent being ... by whom all natural things
are directed to their end", to which Aquinas appends "and this being we
call God" -- an identification by common usage, not a demonstrated
uniqueness claim, exactly the further step the design argument as such
never performs (see
[[the-design-argument-concludes-to-a-maker-not-to-one-maker--202609011016]]).

The closing paragraphs of Grudem's wisdom section make a claim about God that
no event in a life could disturb, and the passage's own construction shows why
that is intended rather than accidental
([[grudem-closes-gods-wisdom-in-the-readers-own-life--202609081645]], from
[[grudem-systematic-theology--202609050158]]). Three features do it. The scope
is total — "all the things that come into our lives" — so no event falls
outside the class the claim is about. The tense is prospective: the assurance is
issued about "even today", a day whose contents are not yet known to the person
being assured. And the worked example is chosen from the adverse side: a thorn
that was prayed against, that stayed, that Grudem calls painful, and whose
*remaining* is scored as an instance of the attribute — "God in his wisdom had
chosen not to remove" it. An argument reading wisdom off the evidence would
have reached for the case where things went well; this one reaches for the case
where they did not, because it is not reading off the evidence at all.

The inference runs opposite to the way the design arguments this base has
mapped run. There, features of the world are the premises and a designer is the
conclusion, and the whole difficulty is how much the premises will carry
([[the-design-argument-concludes-to-a-maker-not-to-one-maker--202609011016]],
[[knowability-doctrine-and-design-arguments-draw-on-the-same-channel--202609011040]]).
Here the conclusion is held first — it arrives as a quoted promise — and the
events are what it is applied to. The wisdom is not detected in the life; it is
predicated of the life on other authority.

This is worth stating plainly because the same structural property has already
been recorded on the other side of the map, and recorded as a cost.
[[the-carbon-tunings-evidential-weight-moved-and-no-belief-moved-with-it--202609022357]]
found belief in the fine-tuning argument holding at constant strength while the
number underneath it moved by a factor of several, and closed by admitting that
such insensitivity is also what an unfalsifiable commitment would produce — and
that the note could not by itself tell the two apart, the discriminator being
"whether the believer can say where the belief does rest, and whether that
ground is itself answerable to anything."

Grudem's passage is a clean instance of the case that note could not supply
from within the fine-tuning literature: a believer saying exactly where the
belief rests, in the text he quotes rather than in the circumstances he applies
it to. Nothing here is offered as evidence and then protected from evidence;
the claim is issued as an application of Rom. 8:28 and the argument for it, such
as it is, is an argument about that verse. So the insensitivity is not
concealed, and the confirm/ground distinction
([[design-arguments-can-confirm-belief-they-do-not-ground--202609011510]]) sorts
the two cases: the fine-tuning argument is a confirming increment that must not
be mistaken for a ground, while this passage is the ground being used, with no
confirming increment claimed. Whether the ground is itself answerable to
anything is a different question, and this passage does not raise it — the
locus would be Rom. 8:28 and the doctrine of providence, neither of which is on
file here.

**Limits.** Grudem is making no epistemological claim in this passage and would
not recognise the vocabulary of this note; he is consoling, and the note reads
the structure of a consolation. He never asks what would count against the
claim, so this note's opening sentence is an inference from the scope of "all
things" and from his treatment of the thorn, not a statement he makes. The
2 Cor. 12 material is cited rather than quoted, so what Paul gave as his own
reason for accepting the thorn is not in the capture. The chapter's argument
that God *is* wise — which may well argue from evidence, and would then sit on
the other side of this contrast within the same section — is not on file, so
this note is about the section's closing use and not about the doctrine of
divine wisdom as Grudem establishes it.

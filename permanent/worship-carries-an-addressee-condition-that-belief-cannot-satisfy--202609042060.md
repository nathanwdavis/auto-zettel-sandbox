Bogardus and Urban leave the question open — there "may be conditions on worship
beyond referring to the right being." Maimonides states one, in a legal code,
without hedging.

"Even if the person worshiping knows that ה' is the [true] God and serves the
creation in the manner in which Enosh and the people of his generation
worshiped [the stars] originally, he is considered to be an idol worshiper" (see
[[the-halachah-makes-the-prohibition-independent-of-what-the-worshipper-knows--202609042056]]).

Read the protasis carefully. It *grants* the thing the same-God dispute fights
over. This worshipper has the reference right; he knows which being is the true
God. He is an idolater anyway, because the condition worship imposes is not on
what he believes but on **whom the act is directed to**. Belief is about a
proposition; worship is about an addressee, and getting the first right does not
settle the second.

Maimonides' history makes the same point in reverse. The first idolaters were
monotheists reasoning correctly from monotheist premises — God made the stars,
God honours His ministers, honouring a king's servants honours the king — and
the knowledge of God survived two full stages of the decline (see
[[maimonides-makes-the-first-idolaters-theists-who-reasoned-about-honour--202609042055]]).
What failed first was not the reference.

Aquinas locates the condition in the same place while drawing the line
elsewhere: latria is owed under the aspect of "the power to create," which "the
creature does not partake of," and dulia is a *different virtue*, not a lesser
worship (see [[aquinas-separates-worship-from-honour-by-the-aspect-under-which-it-is-owed--202609042057]]).
For both, what makes an act worship is the aspect or description under which it
is rendered — and that is a property of the act, not of the agent's beliefs.

So "worship", the word this base has used in some sixty notes without defining,
is not a synonym for "religious behaviour toward the being one refers to." It
names an act with its own addressee condition. That is why
[[the-classical-summons-presupposes-a-shared-acknowledgement-and-disputes-the-worship--202609041215]]
is coherent rather than confused: shared acknowledgement and disputed worship is
exactly what this structure predicts.

The two cells the owner's account puts inside the hidden tier are not two of a kind. One can be worked and the other cannot, and the source of the vocabulary says so.

**The asymmetry.** "it is the latter category that tend to be the difficult ones", Rumsfeld says of the unknown unknowns, and the reason is structural rather than a matter of degree ([[rumsfeld-sorts-what-is-known-into-known-knowns-known--202609151910]]). A known unknown is a question in hand; it can be listed, prioritised, researched, and its answer, when it comes, is recognisable as the answer. This base's inquiry files are an inventory of exactly that. An unknown unknown has no question in hand, so it cannot be listed, and the only statement that can be made about the class is that it is non-empty; any attempt to name a member converts it, in the naming, into a known unknown. So the account's two cells relate to inquiry differently: the first is inquiry's agenda, the second its blind spot, and no amount of work on the first shrinks the second, because the second is defined by not being on the agenda.

**The reflexive cell.** The taxonomy also folds into itself. The statement that there are unknown unknowns is itself a known unknown: a question (what are they?) with no answer. That is the only form in which the second cell is ever present in a discourse, and it is the form Rumsfeld uses. The briefing's follow-up question, whether the case at hand "is an unknown unknown", is answered "I'm not going to say which it is", which is the correct answer, since a named case cannot be one.

**The missing cell.** Socrates supplies a category the account lacks. Testing the oracle on a man reputed wise, he concludes: "he knows nothing, and thinks that he knows. I neither know nor think that I know. In this latter particular, then, I seem to have slightly the advantage of him" ([[socrates-makes-knowing-that-he-does-not-know-the-whole-of--202609151909]]). The politician's state is not a known unknown and not an unknown unknown; it is a false known, an unknown mistaken for a known, and Socrates ranks his own known unknown above it. Transposed to the owner's account, the false known is the class of things believed to have been revealed that were not, and it is the cell in which every heresy, and every misreading of a proof text, sits. Deut. 29:29 read of knowledge when it may be about sins is a candidate. The account has no place for it because its partition starts from what God knows and asks only what he has shared; a false known is not in that set at all. It is in the creature's set, as an error, and an account of what humans know of God needs a cell for what they wrongly think they know.

**What this does to the base's own inventory.** The residues each tradition concedes in the foreknowledge debate ([[the-same-wall-and-the-mystery-put-on-opposite-sides--202609081914]]) are all known unknowns: named questions, deliberately left. That is why they can be compared and their placement argued over. The same debate has no way to see its unknown unknowns, and the false-known cell is where each side puts the other's doctrine.

**Limits.** Rumsfeld is a press briefing and Socrates is a defence speech; neither is a theory of knowledge, and the three-cell vocabulary predates the briefing without any earlier primary source being on file. The Socratic passage is about human wisdom, and its transfer to the knowledge of God is this base's.

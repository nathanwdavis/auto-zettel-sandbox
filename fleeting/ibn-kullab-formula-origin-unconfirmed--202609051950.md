Multiple web sources (Wikipedia, IlmGate blog) attribute the formula "neither identical to nor distinct from the essence" (lā huwa wa-lā ghayruhu) to Ibn Kullab (d. ca. 240/855 CE), stating that al-Ash'ari (d. 324/935) inherited this doctrine from Ibn Kullab's school.

**Status:** Unconfirmed in this base. These sources are general-web tier and not authoritative for technical theological claims. The formula itself does not appear in the al-Ibana passages captured for this cycle (pp. 43, 50, 99-100).

**To verify:** Would require access to:
- al-Ash'ari's Maqalat al-Islamiyyin (Ritter ed., 1929-1933, Arabic) - reports Ibn Kullab's doctrine
- al-Shahrastani's al-Milal wa'l-Nihal - another doxographical source on Ibn Kullab
- Ibn Kullab's own surviving works or early accounts of his theology

**Note for future cycles:** The distinction between Ibn Kullab's originating this position versus al-Ash'ari's development of it is important for understanding the history of Ash'arite theology, but this base currently cannot ground it.

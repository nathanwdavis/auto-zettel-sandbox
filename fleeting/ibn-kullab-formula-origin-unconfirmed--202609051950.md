Multiple web sources (Wikipedia, IlmGate blog) attribute the formula "neither identical to nor distinct from the essence" (lā huwa wa-lā ghayruhu) to Ibn Kullab (d. ca. 240/855 CE), stating that al-Ash'ari (d. 324/935) inherited this doctrine from Ibn Kullab's school.

**Status (updated 2026-09-07): the core is now grounded in the primary text.** The
Maqalat al-Islamiyyin was reached (archive.org DLI scan of the 1930 edition) and its
section *Sharh qawl 'Abd Allah ibn Kullab* (p. 546) does report Ibn Kullab holding
the attributes to be "لا هى هو ولا غيره" (neither He nor other than He). That grounds
the attribution of the formula *to Ibn Kullab as al-Ash'ari reports him* — now carried
by permanent note
[[al-ash-ari-s-maqalat-puts-the-islamic-attributes-at-the--202609072135]] via
reference [[maqalat-al-islamiyyin-wa-ikhtilaf-al-musallin--202609072129]]. The
web-tier sources are no longer load-bearing for that point.

**Residual lead only:** whether Ibn Kullab *originated* the formula or the Ash'arite
school later credited him with a position it systematised is still not settled — the
Maqalat's "*sharh qawl*" frame reports his doctrine, not a chain of transmission.
al-Shahrastani's *al-Milal wa'l-Nihal* or Ibn Kullab's own reported works would be the
route to the origination question, if a later cycle wants it. This note is kept as
that narrowed lead.

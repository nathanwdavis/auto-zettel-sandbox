Own-words summary of the published abstract of [[tachin-do-they-worship-the-same-god--202608311925]]. **The full
article was not retrieved and has not been read**; nothing here should be taken
as a report of its detailed argument.

The abstract states the position and, more usefully, states where it locates the
question. Tachin grants the premise the other side starts from -- that the three
share the same roots in Abraham, which "makes some, including Christian
scholars, conclude that they worship the same God" -- and grants further that
they "may have shared the same broad categories about their supreme deities."
He still concludes that they "actually do not worship the same God," on the
ground that "true worship of God is trinitarian and Christ centered."

The move worth recording is the one the abstract makes explicitly: "the question
turns on the real substance of worship and its approval by God." That shifts the
test away from whether the traditions pick out the same being and onto whether
the worship offered is the right worship and is accepted. Answered that way, a
no follows almost immediately -- which is why participants who agree on the
underlying facts still reach opposite answers.

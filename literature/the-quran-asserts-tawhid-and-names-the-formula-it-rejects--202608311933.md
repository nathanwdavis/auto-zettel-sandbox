Own-words summary of [[quran-tawhid-passages--202608311923]].

Surah 112 states the doctrine positively and negatively at once: God is one, the
eternally besought of all, "He begetteth not nor was begotten," and "there is
none comparable unto Him." The denial of begetting rules out divine sonship
understood as generation, and the denial of comparability rules out any likeness
between God and creatures.

The other two passages are addressed outward rather than inward. 4:171 speaks to
"People of the Scripture," grants that "the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary, was only
a messenger of Allah, and His word which He conveyed unto Mary, and a spirit
from Him," and then says: "say not 'Three' -- Cease!... Allah is only One
Allah." 5:73 states that those who say God "is the third of three" disbelieve.

This matters for the comparison because the disagreement is explicit in the
text, not an artifact of later polemic: the Qur'an names a trinitarian
formulation and rejects it, while continuing to honour Jesus in high terms.

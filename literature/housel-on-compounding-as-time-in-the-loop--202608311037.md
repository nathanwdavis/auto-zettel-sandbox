Own-words summary of one lesson from [[housel-the-psychology-of-money--202608311036]]:
the counterintuitive power of compounding comes less from the rate of return in
any one period than from how long the gains are left in place to earn on
themselves. A merely good return sustained without interruption for a very long
time produces outcomes that outsize intuition, because the base doing the
earning keeps absorbing its own output.

**Grounding: raw/202609151851-rashi-on-deuteronomy-29-28.txt, Rashi's one comment on the verse in Hebrew and in the Rosenbaum-Silbermann English, as served by Sefaria. Read whole. The Talmudic passages the comment cites (Sanhedrin 43b, Sotah 37b) are not captured and are not used.**

**The comment is an answer to an objection.** Rashi frames the verse as Moses' reply to Israel's protest against the threat in the preceding verses (29:17-21 in Hebrew numbering), where one hidden idolater brings plagues on the whole land: "But what can we do? Thou threatenest the many (the whole community) with punishment because of the sinful thoughts of one individual ... But surely no man can know the secret thoughts of his fellow!" The reply distributes liability: "I do not threaten to punish you because of secret thoughts for these belong to the Lord our God and He will exact punishment from that individual; but those things which are revealed belong to us and to our children that we may put away the evil from our midst; and if we do not execute judgment upon them, the whole community will be punished."

**What the two participles mean on this reading.** The concealed things (הנסתרות) are the hidden sins, the "secret thoughts" (הרהורי היחיד), of individuals; the revealed things (הנגלות) are sins committed openly. That they "belong to the Lord our God" means that God alone punishes them; that the revealed things "belong to us and to our children" means that they fall within the community's jurisdiction; and the verse's purpose clause is glossed as the duty to execute judgment, "that we may put away the evil from our midst" (לבער הרע מקרבנו). The verse, so read, is entirely about acts and their punishment. It says nothing about what God knows and humans do not, except incidentally, that God knows hidden sins and the court cannot.

**The extraordinary points.** The dots over "to us and to our children" are read as a further limit on even the community's liability for open sins: "even for the revealed sins (those committed openly), He did not punish the community until they had crossed the Jordan — from the moment when they took upon themselves the oath on Mount Gerizim and Mount Ebal and had thus become responsible for one another." The marked words are made to date the liability, not to expand it.

**Bearing on this base.** This is the reading the JPS translators encode when they render the two participles as concealed and overt *acts* (see the JPS witness in [[deuteronomy-29-29-the-secret-things-and-the-revealed-things--202609151849]]), and it is the Jewish reading that [[deut-29-29-partitions-knowledge-and-grudem-partitions-will--202609081906]] anticipated without a source, when it said that on the Jewish reading the withheld things are God's business and the revealed things are Israel's to do. Rashi confirms that anticipation and sharpens it. The axis of the verse in the rabbinic reading is not disclosure at all but jurisdiction, and the hidden/revealed pair sorts sins, not doctrines.

**Limits.** One commentator. Rashi reports the Talmudic reading (Sanhedrin 43b) rather than inventing it, but this note has not read the Talmud, and later Jewish commentators (Ibn Ezra, Ramban, Sforno) are not on file and may read the verse differently.

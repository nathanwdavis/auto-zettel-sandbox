Own-words summary of [[tikkunei-ha-zohar--202609011531]].

The passage is a prayer, spoken in the text by Elijah, and it is built as a
series of denials about the One addressed followed by a description of the ten
through which that One acts.

**The denials.** "Master of the Worlds! For You are He that is One -- but not
in number." Unity is asserted and immediately removed from arithmetic. Then:
"You are high above all the high-ones, hidden, beyond all that is hidden, no
thought apprehends You at all," and later, "of You, there is no imagining, and
there is no likeness, of anything that is inside or out," closing with "there
is none that knows of You at all." The attribute-denials are formulaic:
"You are 'wise', but not with any specific 'wisdom'. You are He that
'understands', but not from any specific 'understanding'," and of the moral
attributes, "not that You have any specific righteousness... And none of any
of these attributes at all."

**The names.** The sharpest sentence for the same-God question is on 17b:
"all the sephirot, each one has its own name, and with them, angels are
called. But You have no specific Name, for You are He that fills all Names,
and You are the completeness of everything. And when You withdraw from them,
all those names are left like a body without a soul." Names attach to the ten
and to the angels called by them; the One who fills the names bears none.

**The ten and the sin against them.** The sefirot are "ten 'constructs'...
with which to direct hidden worlds that are not revealed, and worlds that are
revealed. And in them You are concealed." They are enumerated with bodily
correspondences -- Chesed the right arm, Gevurah the left, Tiferet the body,
Netzach and Hod the two thighs, Yesod the covenant-sign, Malkhut the mouth
called Oral Torah, with Chokhmah the brain, Binah the heart, and Keter the
crown above. And the offence against them is defined: "You are He that binds
them, and unites them, and, because You are within them, anyone who separates
one from its companion, of these ten, is considered as though he had caused
separation in You."

**The asymmetry.** The ten are directed and do not direct: "You are He that
directs them, and there is none who direct You, -- not above, and not below,
and not from any side." Their names, garments and bodies are given to them, not
had by right. The point of creation is stated in the same key -- heaven, earth,
sun, moon, living things, all made "so that higher-ones would be recognised
through them" -- disclosure through the world, terminating in the admission
that the One so disclosed is still unknown.

Own-words summary of [[maimonides-kings-and-wars-11--202609042021]].

In the halachot the censors cut, Maimonides answers what he calls the greatest
stumbling block. Of Christianity he writes that it "caused the Jews to be slain
by the sword, their remnants to be scattered and humbled, the Torah to be
altered, and the majority of the world to err and **serve a god other than the
Lord**."

That last phrase is the one this repository needs. It is not a complaint about
worship being defective, or about a description being wrong. It is the claim
that the object is wrong — the world was made to serve *a god other than the
Lord*. On the distinction this base has been using, that is a verdict on
reference, and it is negative.

He then withholds the same verdict from Islam. The providential sentence pairs
the two without distinguishing them: "all the deeds of Jesus of Nazareth and
that Ishmaelite who arose after him will only serve to prepare the way for
Mashiach's coming and the improvement of the entire world, motivating the
nations to serve God together." Islam is named — obliquely, as "that Ishmaelite"
— and carries none of the charge laid against Christianity in the preceding
sentence.

The asymmetry is therefore visible twice over in one passage: an accusation of
serving another god, made against Christianity only, inside an argument whose
conclusion treats both religions as instruments of the same end.

**A limit.** This is a Messianic chapter, not a treatise on idolatry, and the
charge is embedded in a polemic about what Christianity did to Israel. It is
evidence of where Maimonides put the line, not a worked argument for putting it
there.

Own-words summary of the published abstract of [[vroom-do-all-religious-traditions-worship-the-same-god--202608311926]]. **The full
article was not retrieved and has not been read.** The abstract says the author
elaborates his own view in the final section but does not say what that view is,
so no conclusion may be attributed to him here.

The abstract's contribution is a taxonomy. Analysing arguments that affirm and
deny that all religions worship the same God, it reports that they are of
different types: some rest on the author's theological insights; some appeal to
the results of comparative religion; a third kind derives from philosophy of
religion; and a fourth from philosophical views about the ultimate unity of the
world. The paper then asks which kind of argument is decisive.

That question -- not which answer is right, but which discipline is entitled to
settle it -- is a good description of why the dispute is durable.

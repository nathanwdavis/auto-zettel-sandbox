Own-words summary of [[chalcedon-definition--202609041958]].

Read for what it settles, the Chalcedonian definition is about the constitution
of Christ, not about the internal articulation of God. Its subject throughout is
"one and the same Christ" in two natures; its opponents are those who divide him
into two or confound the natures into one. It reaffirms the creeds of 325 and
381 rather than adding to them on the doctrine of God.

Recorded here mainly for what follows from that. Inquiry 202609041841 asked
whether the conciliar record supports a claim about the persons being more than
relations *ad extra*, and named Chalcedon as one of the places to look. It is
not there. Chalcedon is doing christology: the question it answers is how deity
and humanity stand in one subject, not how Father, Son and Spirit stand to one
another. A search of the definition for the *ad extra* question returns the
council's reaffirmation of Nicaea and nothing further.

This is a negative result and is filed as one, so that the next reader does not
spend the fetch.

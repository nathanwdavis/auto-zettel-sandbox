Own-words summary of [[sanhedrin-91b-antoninus-and-rabbi-on-when-the-evil--202610032104]].

Antoninus puts two questions to Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi, and in both Rabbi gives
way to him and then finds a verse for the answer.

The first asks when the soul is placed in a person, at conception or at the
formation of the embryo. Rabbi says formation; Antoninus argues that unsalted
meat would rot in three days, so the soul must be there from conception. Rabbi
accepts this and cites Job 10:12.

The second asks "From when does the evil inclination dominate a person?" -- from
the formation of the embryo or from emergence from the womb. Rabbi again says
formation. Antoninus objects that the inclination would then make the fetus
"kick his mother's innards and emerge from the womb", so "the evil inclination
dominates a person from the moment of emergence from the womb". Rabbi accepts
this too, citing Genesis 4:7, "Sin crouches at the entrance", which the
translation's gloss takes as the entrance of the mother's womb. The verse's
word for sin (חַטָּאת) is thereby read as the evil inclination.

The question in both exchanges is one of timing: when a soul is present and
when an inclination begins to rule. The two segments say nothing about guilt
or its inheritance.

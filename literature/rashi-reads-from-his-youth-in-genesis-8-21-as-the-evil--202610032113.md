Own-words summary of [[rashi-on-genesis-8-21-from-his-youth--202610032105]].

Rashi's first comment is on the word minne'urav, from his youth, in God's
statement that the imagination of man's heart is evil from his youth. He notes that the word
is written defectively, without a vav, which lets it be read from the root for
stirring; on that reading, in the Silbermann translation, "from the moment the
embryo bestirs itself to have an independent existence the evil inclination is
given to it". The Hebrew is more concrete: from when it stirs to come out of
its mother's womb (לָצֵאת מִמְּעֵי אִמּוֹ), the evil inclination (יֵצֶר הָרָע)
is given in it. He gives Genesis Rabbah 34:10 as his source.

So Rashi reads the verse as fixing the start of the evil inclination at birth.
He speaks of an inclination given, not of a sin committed or a guilt
inherited.

The second comment, on God's doubled promise in the same verse, concerns the
oath to Noah and is not about sin.

This is an unattributed lesson outline presented on waynegrudem.com accompanying the teaching of Chapter 14 (The Trinity). The PDF carries metadata showing creation on 2009-03-18 by Mike Langley; the site lists a printed date of 2006/08/27 beside the lecture audio link. The page numbers and section headings that follow match the 1994 edition's pagination as the other witnesses print it; the outline itself names no edition.

The outline states the Trinity definition at page 226, then at pages 231-241 presents three component statements: "God is three persons"; "Each person is fully God"; "There is one God." At pages 241-248, the outline discusses historical errors. Modalism is described as a claim "that there is one person who appears to us in three different forms (or 'modes')," with the note that its shortcoming is denying the personal relationships within the Trinity and denying the independence of God. Arianism is noted to deny the full deity of the Son and the Holy Spirit. Tritheism is noted to deny that there is only one God. The outline does not explicitly pair each error to the denial of a single statement, though the structure suggests each error involves rejection of one of the three biblical claims.

In section D)2)a) (page 248), the outline frames the relationship between the three persons of the Trinity and God's single being as "ontological equality but economic subordination," meaning the personal distinctions in the Trinity are not something added onto God's real being, and the three persons are not just three different ways of looking at the one being of God. Rather, there are three distinct persons with being equal to the whole being of God, and the only distinction between them lies in the ways they relate to each other and to creation.

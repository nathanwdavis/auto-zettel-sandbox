**Grounding: the raw capture of the Or HaChaim's comment, Hebrew and Munk's English, from the Sefaria API, read whole.**

The comment opens by paraphrasing the verse: "The hidden sins are G'd's business, but the visible ones (are our business to deal with)." It then assigns the verse a speaker. The words are not Moses' but Israel's: "the response by the Jewish people to Moses that he cannot hold them responsible for sins committed by fellow Jews in private. However, they accept the responsibility to try and stop sins being committed in public." The Hebrew names what is accepted: קִיְּמוּ וְקִבְּלוּ עֲלֵיהֶם הָעַרְבוּת, they confirmed and took upon themselves the mutual responsibility. And "for us and our children forever" is read as the formal acceptance of the oath: "This is the official acceptance by the Israelites of the oath Moses wanted them to swear."

**What this adds.** Two things not in the earlier commentators. The verse becomes dialogue, Israel's answer to the covenant demand of the preceding verses, which is the same frame Rashi uses when he casts the verse as a reply to Israel's protest that it cannot know its fellows' secret thoughts. And the accepted thing is named, *arvut*, the mutual responsibility that Sanhedrin 43b dates to the crossing of the Jordan. The reading is wholly about liability; the hidden sins are those "committed by fellow Jews in private".

**Limits.** One comment; Munk's translation undated on Sefaria; the earlier verse (29:13) the Hebrew cross-refers to is not captured.

Own-words summary of [[sukkah-52a-52b-the-names-of-the-evil-inclination-and-the--202610061550]].

Four segments of Sukkah 52a-52b on the evil inclination.

Its end. Rabbi Yehuda teaches that at the end of days "God will bring the evil
inclination and slaughter it" before the righteous and the wicked. The righteous
see it as a high mountain and weep that they overcame it; the wicked see it as a
hair and weep that they did not.

Its names. "The evil inclination has seven names". The first is God's: "The Holy
One, Blessed be He, called it evil", from Genesis 8:21. The captured segment
gives the next two, Moses's (uncircumcised, Deut 10:16) and David's (impure, by
inference from Ps 51:12); the other four are in segments not captured.

Its maker's regret. In the school of Rav, there are "four creations that the
Holy One, Blessed be He, created, yet He, as it were, regrets that He created
them", and the evil inclination is the fourth. Its proof text is Micah 4:6, "her
that I corrupted", which the translation glosses: "He created the evil
inclination that led the people to sin".

Across the four segments the inclination is something God made, named and
regrets, and will himself destroy. No segment traces it to Adam.

Short verbatim excerpts from Wayne Grudem, Systematic Theology: An Introduction
to Biblical Doctrine, 2nd ed. (Zondervan Academic, 2020), chapter 13, "The
Character of God: 'Communicable' Attributes (Part 2)", section D "Attributes of
Purpose", items 14 (Will), 15 (Freedom) and 16 (Omnipotence)
(reference: grudem-systematic-theology--202609050158).

PROVENANCE. The repository owner (nathanwdavis) supplied this section as a
15-page PDF in a Claude Code session on 2026-09-08, exported from their own
licensed copy in Logos Bible Study, with the same running attribution to the
second edition that the ch. 16 export carries. About 4,500 words; read entire by
the session before any note was written. Fourth owner-supplied capture of the
second edition, after the ch. 12 omniscience export (raw/202609061910), the pp.
221-222 spirituality drop (raw/202609071342) and the ch. 16 providence export
(raw/202609081823).

PAGE NUMBERS. The export prints no book folios. Two of the section's own
footnotes cite chapter 16 by book page: God's decrees at "pp. 440-42" and the
relation of God's will to evil at "pp. 428-34". Both overlap the anchors the
ch. 16 capture yielded from that chapter's internal references (pp. 440-41 and
pp. 428-33) and extend each by a page; the difference is recorded and nothing
in this repository turns on it. "Export p. N" below is the PDF page, 1-15.

TEXT RECONSTRUCTION. Same defect as the ch. 16 export, plus one slot that
chapter did not use. The embedded font subsets map the ligatures "Th", "tt" and
"ft" onto ASCII glyph slots, here ! " # and -- new -- & ("&ere" for There,
"&ough" for Though), with U+FFFE for line-break hyphenation. A full sweep for
any non-alphanumeric character adjacent to letters was run over BOTH this export
and the ch. 16 export before reconstruction; it found the & slot here and
confirmed the ch. 16 text contains no &, so that earlier capture is unaffected.
All 22 affected word-forms here were resolved individually, and every passage
below was then checked as an exact substring of the reconstruction.

EXTENT. This is NOT the full section. The book is under copyright and this
repository is public, so only the passages this repository's notes cite are
retained: 31 of them. (Two were added on a second pass: the passage-locating
step matched quotations against single export pages, so the two that straddle a
page break -- the definition of God's will, and the naming of the will of
precept -- were silently dropped and then caught by a check of every quotation
the notes use against the capture.) This capture was assembled AFTER the notes were written,
from the quotations they actually use -- the procedure the ch. 16 ingest arrived
at the hard way, having written its capture first and then found sixteen cited
passages missing from it.

CONTENTS NOT RETAINED. The section's fuller scriptural surveys under each
heading; the treatment of God's omnipotence beyond its definition and the
"cannot" list; and the chapter's promised material on the summary attributes
(perfection, blessedness, beauty, glory), which the opening paragraph announces
but the export does not reach.

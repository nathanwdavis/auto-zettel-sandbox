HOW EXHAUSTION WAS ESTABLISHED, AND WHY THE PREVIOUS RULE WAS WRONG. This base's
standing access note said a page with a successor carries a `التالي` control.
That is true of page 1 and of page 1 only: interior pages render the same
forward control with the ENGLISH alt `Next`, and — the part that matters — the
control is served INDEFINITELY, on empty pages past the end of a section as
well as inside it. Testing for `التالي` therefore stops every section at two
pages, and testing for either alt never stops at all. Neither control is
evidence. What is evidence is the text: every page of a section reprints that
section's verse as one long line, and a page carrying commentary carries more
than that one line. Each section below was read until a page came back with the
verse alone, and the TWO pages after the last content page were then fetched and
are recorded, so that a single empty page inside a section could not have ended
the read early. The rule and this capture's page counts are what the accompanying
INBOX entry reports.

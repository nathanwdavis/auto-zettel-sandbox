capture answers both: it is a Zaydi fiqh text, and it names al-Hadi and al-Qasim for that
reading in its own voice.

WHAT IT CONTAINS. The matn of the Azhar (al-Mahdi Ahmad b. Yahya al-Murtada, d. 840/1437)
requires the spouses to agree "fi al-milla"; the commentary grounds the prohibition in
Q 2:221 and Q 60:10, and disposes of Q 5:5 by INTERPRETATION rather than abrogation — the
"muhsanat min alladhina utu al-kitab" are read as those of the People of the Book WHO
BELIEVED. It then records the dissent: al-Sadiq, al-Baqir, Abu Hanifa and al-Shafi'i permit
marrying kitabiyyat, and al-Intisar chose that view and called it the consensus of the first
generation.

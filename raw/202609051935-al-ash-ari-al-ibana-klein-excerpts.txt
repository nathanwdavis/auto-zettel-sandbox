AL-IBANA PROPER (Klein's Translation)
================

PASSAGE 1: al-Ash'ari's PREFACE TO AL-IBANA — GOD'S ETERNAL ATTRIBUTES
Source: al-Ibana 'an Usul al-Diyana, Klein's translation
Printed page: p. 43
Genre: al-Ash'ari's own text in al-Ibana

From al-Ash'ari (Klein translation, p. 43):

"He has always had the attributes of primacy and power, and He will always
continue to be knowing and cognizant. His knowledge embraces created things,
His will is fully realized in them, and the secrets of things are not far
from Him. The vicissitudes of passing time do not alter Him, nor does fatigue
or weariness overtake Him in the creation of anything He creates, nor does
exhaustion or loss of power touch Him."

================
PASSAGE 2: al-Ash'ari ON GOD'S NAMES NOT OTHER THAN HE
Source: al-Ibana 'an Usul al-Diyana, Klein's translation
Printed page: p. 50
Genre: al-Ash'ari's own text in al-Ibana

From al-Ash'ari (Klein translation, p. 50):

"and that anybody who thinks that the names of God are other than He is in
error; and that God has knowledge (as He said, 'in His knowledge He sent it
down,' and as He said, 'and no female conceiveth or bringeth forth without
His knowledge'). We also assert that God has hearing and sight, and we do not
deny it as the Mutazilah, the Jahmiyyah, and the Harijites deny it; and we
assert that God has prowess (quwwah) (as He has said, 'Saw they not that God
Who created them was mightier than they in prowess?')."

================
PASSAGE 3: al-Ash'ari REPORTS MU'TAZILI POSITION ON ATTRIBUTES
Source: al-Ibana 'an Usul al-Diyana, Klein's translation
Printed page: pp. 99-100
Genre: al-Ash'ari's own text in al-Ibana (Questions and Answers section)

From al-Ash'ari (Klein translation, pp. 99-100):

"Question. The Mu'taziiah deny the attributes of the Lord of the Worlds, and
think that the meaning of 'hearing, seeing,' is identical with the meaning of
'knowing,' just as the Christians think that God's hearing is His sight and
His visibility and His Word and His knowledge and His Son. May God be very
far above that! It may be said to the Mu'taziiah: Since you think that
'hearing, seeing' mean 'knower,' do you not agree that 'wielder of power'
[p. 46] means 'knower'? Therefore, since you think that the meaning of
'hearing, seeing' is 'wielder of power,' do you not think that the meaning of
'wielder of power' is 'knower'? And therefore, since you think that 'living
one' means 'wielder of power,' why do you not think that 'wielder of power'
means 'knower'? Wherefore, if they say, 'In that case everything known must
be an object of power,' the answer is: If 'hearing, seeing' meant 'knower,'
every known thing would certainly be heard; but since that is impossible,
your belief is false."

AL-ASH'ARI'S CREED FROM MAQALAT AL-ISLAMIYYIN
(Quoted in Klein's Introductory Essay, pp. 31-33)
================

PASSAGE 4: al-Ash'ari's CREED — PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTES WITH "BILA KAYFA"
Source: al-Ash'ari's creed as preserved in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, quoted in Klein's introductory essay
Printed page: p. 31-32
Genre: al-Ash'ari's creed text from Maqalat, not al-Ibana translation

From al-Ash'ari's creed (quoted in Klein essay, pp. 31-32):

"II. God is upon His throne, as He has said, 'The Merciful is seated on
The Throne.' He has two hands, bila kayfa, as He has said, 'I have created
with My two hands,' and as He has said, 'Nay! outstretched are both His
hands;' and He has two eyes, bila kayfa, as He has said, 'Under Our eyes
it floated on;' and He has a face, as He has said, 'And the face of thy
Lord shall abide resplendent with majesty and glory.'"

================
PASSAGE 5: al-Ash'ari's CREED — DIVINE NAMES NOT OTHER THAN GOD
Source: al-Ash'ari's creed as preserved in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, quoted in Klein's introductory essay
Printed page: p. 32-33
Genre: al-Ash'ari's creed text from Maqalat, not al-Ibana translation

From al-Ash'ari's creed (quoted in Klein essay, pp. 32-33):

"III. It is not to be said that the names of God are anything but Himself,
as the Mu'tazilah and the Harijites believe. The Sunnites confess that God
has knowledge, as He has said, 'In His knowledge He sent it down,' and as
He has said, 'And no female conceiveth or bringeth forth without His knowledge.'"

================
PASSAGE 6: al-Ash'ari's CREED — SUNNITES ASSERT HEARING, SIGHT, POWER
Source: al-Ash'ari's creed as preserved in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, quoted in Klein's introductory essay
Printed page: p. 33
Genre: al-Ash'ari's creed text from Maqalat, not al-Ibana translation

From al-Ash'ari's creed (quoted in Klein essay, p. 33):

"IV. They assert the existence of His hearing and sight; and they do not
deny that those things belong to God, as the Mu'tazilah do. They assert
that God has prowess (quwwah), as He has said, 'Saw they not that God Who
created them was mightier than they in prowess?'"

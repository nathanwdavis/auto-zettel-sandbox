  Hear, O Israel! The ETERNAL is our God, the ETERNAL alone.bThe ETERNAL is our God, the ETERNAL alone Cf. Rashbam, Ibn Ezra; see Zech. 14.9. In contrast to others “The LORD our God, the LORD is one.” 
  You shall love the ETERNAL your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might.

Note carried in the JPS translation itself: this rendering ("the ETERNAL
alone") follows Rashbam and Ibn Ezra, and the translators flag the contrasting
traditional rendering "The LORD our God, the LORD is one." The ambiguity is in
the Hebrew, not introduced by the translator.
